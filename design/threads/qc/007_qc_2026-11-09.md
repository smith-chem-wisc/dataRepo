# 007 - qc to dataRepo - 2026-11-09 - qctemplates 0.4.0: T1's TSV gains 10 columns for five per-run retention-time numbers, and reports may gain two figures (F13, F14); nothing else in the outputs moves

## What changes for you

- **qctemplates 0.4.0 is merged** (qc `4c04a41`) and live in PXReprise's QC stage. It adds five optional per-run
  numbers from MSL's retention-time calibration (our D15): M14 and M15.
- **`T1_file_summary.tsv` gains 10 columns in every report from now on**, even when the payload has none of the new
  numbers (those cells are then empty).
  - Five value columns come right after `contaminant_intensity_frac`: `rt_fit_sd`, `rt_min_to_irt50`,
    `rt_void_per_1k`, `rt_double_peak_per_1k` and `rt_streak_per_1k`.
  - Their five `__status` columns come right after `contaminant_intensity_frac__status`.
  - **Read by name, nothing changes. Read by position, every `__status` column moves 5 places, and `outlier_file` and
    `excluded` move 10.**
- **T2, T3 and T4 are unchanged.**
  - T2 can now list findings on the new metrics: `metric_id` M14 or M15, with rules like "warn > 10 iRT (MSL 008,
    provisional)".
  - Checked on PXD006898: 0.3.0 and 0.4.0 write byte-identical T2-T4.
- **Figures.** `F13_rt_calibration` and `F14_lc_artifacts` (`.svg` and `.png`) appear only when a payload carries
  the new numbers, so a report can now have up to 14 figures.
- **No payload carries them yet.** PXReprise has been asked to fill them (our PXR-C12).
