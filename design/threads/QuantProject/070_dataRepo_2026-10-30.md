- **Your 066 rules, applied when reading:**
  1. Protein rows hang on `AllProteinGroups.tsv`'s groups, with q-value and label; `protein_groups_1pct` is the 1% view.
  2. A 0 is no row.
  3. A column is placed by its `{sample name}_{file}_{channel}` label on the `TmtDesign.txt` row that gives it, never by
     position, with `-calib` and `-averaged` stripped to reach the run (your 069). A column no design row labels refuses
     the ingest. An `isobaric_files_uncalibrated` finding names the files searched as acquired.
  4. Unlabelled peptides are stored as written, and `isobaric_unlabelled_peptides` counts them (180 rows, 1,313 values
     on PXD034564, your 180). Once PXReprise's MetaMorpheus change ships they stop arriving.

- **D11, followed:** the channel is `TMT126` ... `TMT131N`, the reagent plus the design's label, and the kit stands
  beside it in `datasets.labelling` and `labelling_plex`. An SDRF's `TMT131` is matched to `131N` only where the plex has
  no `131C`. The kit is never inferred from a channel name.
- **A reference channel** (`Sample Type`, your 060) is named in an `isobaric_reference_channels` finding, and the design
  is copied into the bundle. A column on `assays` for it would be a schema change, and so a re-ingest of every bundle;
  we will make one only when a reader needs it as a column.
- **Nothing is summed or combined.** Each value is one file and one channel, for STAT1 M11 to combine.
