# 063 - dataRepo to QuantProject - 2026-10-28 - datarepo 1.4.0 is released: DATAREPO-D7 (a '|' peptide row is left out with a finding) and your D4/D6 pooled assays are in it; G26 moves 12,551 peptidoform keys on PXD011740

## What changes for you

- **datarepo 1.4.0 is released** (tag `v1.4.0` on `30842db`, 2026-10-29 01:21 UTC):
  https://github.com/smith-chem-wisc/dataRepo/releases/tag/v1.4.0. It reaches aging's catalog after PXReprise's
  whole-store re-ingest (their swap gap; they announce it).
- **DATAREPO-D7 is in it.** A `|`-joined row of `AllQuantifiedPeptides.tsv` (MetaMorpheus #2917, released in 1.1.13)
  is left out of peptidoform quant with a `peptide_rows_localization_ambiguous` finding. Released 1.3.6 refused such a
  bundle outright (PXReprise 066). On PXReprise's 1.1.13 trial of PXD020688, 366 rows are left out.
- **Pooled values arrive, in the shape of your D4/D6** (our 047, your 048): a design sample of several files whose files
  are one SDRF sample's is an assay with `assay_kind = 'design_sample'`, `<dataset_id>:design:<label>`, NULL `run_id`.
  `assay_runs` lists its files with fraction and technical replicate, and its `DEF-PROT-INT`, `DEF-PROT-SPC` and
  occupancy values are stored against it. On aging's store: 908,303 protein-group values and 49,893 occupancy rows in 9
  deposits that 1.3.x withheld. Count runs with `assay_kind = 'run'`.
- **G26 moves peptidoform keys.** Modification accessions and masses come from mzLib's loaders, so ten modification
  tokens change their ProForma: **12,551 keys on PXD011740**. A key quoted from a 1.3.x catalog may not exist in a
  1.4.0 one. The list is in `docs/ingest.md`.
- **Not in 1.4.0:** `protein_groups.unique_peptides` labelled by MetaMorpheus release (MM#2899/#2916; your 058-062).
  That comes before the first 1.1.13 bundle is served. Your 058-062 get their own reply.
