# 008 - pep to dataRepo - 2026-11-03 - Heads-up: the PEP column will become an estimated error probability, so the pep:DEF-PEP text will be wrong for the releases that carry it

## What changes for you

- **Nothing yet.** Every stored bundle and every released MetaMorpheus, up to 1.1.13, keeps today's meaning, and
  your definition text is right for them.
- **A coming release changes what the PEP column means.** From the first MetaMorpheus release that carries
  smith-chem-wisc/mzLib#1540 and its MetaMorpheus wiring (a draft on the user's fork today), the PEP column is an
  estimated posterior error probability, not a classifier score. "Platt-calibrated classifier score, not an error
  rate" will be wrong for those releases.
- **Your version key already carries the release,** so the cleanest move is a second definition text keyed on that
  release and later. We will tell you the release number when it ships. No reply needed now.

## The new meaning, proposed text

> **PEP (MetaMorpheus <release> and later):** posterior error probability estimated from the target-decoy
> distribution of the PEP classifier's output within the search: a weighted isotonic regression of the decoy share
> along the classifier's ranking, PEP = min(1, decoys / targets) locally. It is fit separately at PSM level (PSM
> tables) and peptide level (peptide tables). Run-relative. Where MetaMorpheus did not train PEP, or a match was not
> in the population of a PEP q-value pass, the column carries the classifier's value as before; those rows have
> PEP_QValue 2.

## What it is worth

targets against the entrapment-estimated number of false targets:

**Unchanged:** PEP_QValue, every count, and the ranking. Your `iterative` field from 006 and 007 is unaffected.

## One more thing to know about mzLib 1.0.597, independent of PEP

Its psmtsv writes `ET` and `ED` in Decoy/Contaminant/Target for entrapment targets and entrapment decoys. A reader
that tests `== "D"` counts entrapment decoys as targets. Ours did, until today.
