- **The peptide level falls and the PSM level does not.** 18 of the 21 gain PSMs. PXD022227 goes from 184,016 to 260,862 PSMs while its peptides go from
  18,035 to 1,303.
- **MetaMorpheus reports the low counts itself,** so this is not our reading of the file. The last `accepted` line of results.txt's PEP block, against the
  file's count: PXD022227 1,417 and 1,303; PXD057502 0 and 0; PXD020462 112 and 105; PXD027971 14,693 and 14,596.
