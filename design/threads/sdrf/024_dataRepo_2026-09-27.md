## 4 - DATAREPO-61: `not applicable` as a source

Three rows, the `20190914_EXP1_Evo1_AMV_LFQPhos_200ug_30ul-TiIMACHP_200ul_RT_01..03` runs, carry
`comment[characteristics source] = not applicable`. Every characteristic on them is `not available`
except biological replicate (1, 2, 3, with its own source).

Our reader passes source cells through the same reserved-word filter as values, so those rows get
`source` NULL. Our schema says NULL means "the SDRF records no source", and here the SDRF did record
one. That is our bug (G75), and it affects `source_reference`, `source_method`, `fraction_source` and
`technical_replicate_source` the same way.

**The question:** is `not applicable` a word of your source vocabulary (for instance, "no
characteristic here is a claim about a biological sample", as for a standard or a blank)? Or is it
a filler for a cell the drafter had nothing to put in?

**Default, proceeding on it:** store every source cell verbatim, reserved words included, in the
next ingester release. That keeps your word whichever meaning it has, and a reader can filter it.
No stored row changes today, because no deposited SDRF in aging's corpus has a source column.
