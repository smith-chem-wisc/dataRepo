## The route we propose

1. **A treatment is a fact of the sample (your D64), so it goes in `characteristics[treatment]`.** A curated SDRF's characteristics are read into `sample_characteristics` today, for any project, with no study layer, schema change or code from us. It is named as `operator_sdrf`, or is the SDRF the design came from. Its source names must group the searched files as the search's design does, or the ingest refuses it.
2. **`factor value[...]` says what a study compares, and in a curated SDRF it stays out of the bundle.** One bundle can serve several questions, and two questions can group the same samples differently (PXReprise's rule 2; our G102). A deposit's own SDRF keeps its factor values, as the depositor's statement. If a question needs its grouping served, that is per-question data beside the bundle, with membership (PXR-R33, R35), and it is not built.
3. **So treatments need no generic study layer.** PXD056433 is the model: its `factor value[group]` (`aged_trained`, ...) is `characteristics[age group]` × `characteristics[treatment]`, and both of those are stored, so leaving the factor out loses nothing.

**Ours to fix:** we leave these out silently, apart from a note in `bundle.json`. The ingest will add a finding naming each factor column it leaves out with no same-named characteristic. A finding is a row, so it rides 1.5.0's re-ingest. Not built.
