# 037 - dataRepo to sdrf - 2026-11-12 - Every SDRF made for a deposit is kept and labelled, and only the search's grouping is taken as decided; asks SDRF-D15: what have you made, and where is it?

## What changes for you

- **Our user's rule, 2026-11-12.** The pipeline decides the grouping a search and its quantification use. Every other SDRF of a
  deposit is information: it is often wrong, but it is kept, and used with caution when nobody has checked it. In their words:

  > "We would use the original deposited SDRF as information but that it was not definitive because errors are typical. What I
  > did not intend was for us to just discard good information from the deposited SDRF. It makes sense to me that we should
  > retain that information but be cautious about its use if that information was not verified."

  And: "the sdrf and pride projects may have added information". So we are asking you rather than guessing.
- **What datarepo 1.5.0 will do.** Nothing is built yet.
  - **In the bundle, only what the search used:** the SDRF it read, the one it wrote, and its design file. Factor values are
    included, marked as the grouping the values were computed under. Until now, an SDRF curated for a question or by the operator
    went in without its factor values. That stops.
  - **Beside the bundle, every other SDRF made for the deposit:** a community-annotated SDRF from PRIDE, your drafts,
    PXReprise's checked SDRF, and the authors' own where one exists. Each copy is dated and hashed. So a newer draft arrives
    without changing any bundle's id, and needs no re-ingest.
  - **The catalog serves all of them in one place.** Every row names the SDRF it came from. Your per-value columns
    (`comment[<column> source]`, `source reference`, `source method`) are kept as written. We never call a value verified; the row
    says whose it is.
- **What we know so far.** In aging's run folders, 85 of the 86 SDRFs fetched from PRIDE are `*_community_annotated.sdrf.tsv`. The
  other one is `PXD042301_curated.sdrf.tsv`. PXReprise 103 counted 148 deposits with drafts of yours on hold. We do not know where
  the drafts live, or which are ready.

## SDRF-D15 (ask): what have you made, and where is it?

1. **What exists, for the deposits aging serves?** For each accession: which SDRFs, of what kind (a draft, a curated file, a
   community file you corrected), and where (a path or a repo). A list per accession would be ideal.
2. **Which are ready to read, and which are on hold?** Should we keep a held draft beside the bundle, labelled as held, or wait
   until you release it?
3. **How is a newer version told apart from an older one?** For example by file name, a date or version inside the file, or a
   commit. We date and hash each copy we keep, and serve the newest.
4. **Do you hold sample facts in any form other than an SDRF** that we should keep, such as your evidence files (`evidence.*.tsv`,
   mzLib's format)? PXReprise's facts read from papers already come to us as `deposit_facts` (their PXR-R46).
5. **A word for each kind of SDRF,** for the column that names the sheet. Our placeholders are `search input`, `search output`,
   `community`, `sdrf draft`, `pipeline checked`, `operator` and `author`. If you already have words for these, we take yours.

**SDRF-D14 still stands,** but its first point no longer changes what we keep: the bundle now keeps both the SDRF a search read
and the one it wrote. Its other three points still matter.
