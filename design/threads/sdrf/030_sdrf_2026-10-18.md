# 030 - sdrf to dataRepo - 2026-10-18 - Drafted cells can now come from a deposit's own design table: `deposited` with a source reference, and two new reference columns (mzLib #1483)

## What changes for you

1. **Drafts now read the design tables deposits hold.** mzLib #1483 (open, in review) reads three formats:
   Spectronaut's condition setup, an MSstats annotation and MaxQuant's experimental design. The draft takes from them
   the condition, biological replicate, fraction, technical replicate and sample name.
2. **The source word is `deposited`, with a reference.** Such a cell gets `comment[<column> source]` = `deposited`,
   `comment[<column> source reference]` = the file and cell (`metadata/..._ConditionSetup.tsv!R4C6`), and
   `comment[<column> source method]` = `rules`. Your schema keeps `source`, `source_reference` and `source_method`
   verbatim, so the ingest needs no change. One thing to know when you read `deposited`: until now it meant a cell of
   a deposited SDRF, which carries no reference. A `deposited` cell WITH a reference came from a deposited design
   table that names the file.
3. **Two new columns can appear:** `comment[fraction identifier source reference]` and
   `comment[technical replicate source reference]`, each with its `source method`. They sit beside your
   `fraction_source` and `technical_replicate_source` (SDRF-DR10) and say where the value was read. They appear
   wherever a fraction or technical replicate came from a design table or from publication evidence.
4. **Measured:** on four MassIVE deposits with a curated SDRF and a design table, biological replicate agreement goes
   from 0.17 to 0.75 and condition grouping (adjusted Rand index) from 0.09 to 0.72. No cached PRIDE draft changes
   without evidence (1,148 checked).
5. **When.** Through drafts only, after #1483 merges and a release carries it. We will say when.

A notice, not a question.
