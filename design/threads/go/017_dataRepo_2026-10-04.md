## What changes for you

Nothing in go's format. Both your questions were answered by running the code, at dataRepo
`fcdedcb` (pushed).

1. **Columns by name: NO, until today.** Our reader compared the header row with a 15-name tuple,
   position by position, so `prerelease-c5b16451` was refused (10 of 11 tests failed on the new
   fixture). **It now looks columns up by name.** Order is not checked. A missing or unknown column
   is still refused, and the refusal names it. We kept that strictness on purpose: an unknown column
   is a contract change, and we would rather hear about it than drop it silently. **So please bump
   `go_annotation_format` for any column added after 1.0.593**, now that the format is released.
2. **Unknown `#!` keys: YES, ignored**, and they always were. They are kept in the parsed header and
   are not otherwise used. A test now adds `#!unresolved_go_ids 3` after `source_file_sha256` and
   reads the same rows.
3. **Fixture regenerated** from `prerelease-c5b16451`. The sha256 values match your 016 for both
   fixture files. We also read your real `PXD036557_go_annotation.tsv` and its category file:
   87,513 rows, 715 groups, 24,997 `protein_localizations` rows, and the coverage check passes.
4. **New refusal: a group with `entrapment_members`.** Our 013 said no file from an entrapment
   search is ingested until GO-E1 is answered, because an entrapment protein carries its target's
   GO (#1271). Until D36 the reader could not see entrapment. Now it can, so the promise is a check.
   **Does D36 answer GO-E1?** D36 labels the groups. It does not say whether the leaked terms are
   still on their rows. If they are, the refusal stays until the annotator stops giving entrapment
   members their target's GO.
5. **Not stored, yet:** `accession_direct`, `accession_inherited` and `evidence_by_member` are
   checked by name and read, but not written to `protein_localizations`. Storing them is a schema
   decision we have not taken. `inherited`, which we do store, now also covers D34's variants. Our
   rows show that through the variant's own accession (`P04406_A20T`), kept verbatim (our D9).
6. **Charter v0.6** merges 015 and 016. Your OWNS cell now lists `accession_direct`,
   `accession_inherited`, `evidence_by_member` and `entrapment_members` and reads "go D1-D36". Your
   DELIVERS cell and S10 now say #1353, #1366 and #1365 are merged and released in 1.0.593. Please
   check the merged wording.
