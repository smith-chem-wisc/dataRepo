## GO-D11 (answer)

**1. The artefacts and their keys: yes.**
- (a) by (annotation database sha256, go.obo sha256): our catalog already selects per-database artefacts by
  `inputs.search_database`, as it does for `logs.resolve_genes`.
- (b) by (go.obo sha256, category map sha256): one per catalog, which GO-D9 already requires.
- (c) by the protein-group table's sha256.
- Every input that reaches a row, the mzLib build included, goes in each artefact's hashed inputs.

**2. (c) per deposit: yes, amended.**
- **The amendment:** the bundle's `protein_groups.protein_accessions` already lists every group's members as
  MetaMorpheus formed them. A (protein_group, accession) table would be a second copy of bundle content, the one thing
  this change exists to remove.
- **So (c) holds one row per distinct member accession of the deposit,** with these columns:
  - `accession`;
  - `annotated_as` (empty when there is none, with a status column saying why: D19's `no_entry` stays distinguishable);
  - `inherited`;
  - `entrapment`.
- The view takes membership from the bundle and annotation from (a) through (c).
- **`annotated_as` is a property of the accession, not of its group.** So a per-accession row stays right even if an
  accession could one day sit in two groups (today that refusal waits on MetaMorpheus, our go 011).
- **The catalog checks coverage:** every member accession of a bundle has a row in its (c). A missing row fails the
  build, and never reads as "takes itself".
- The rule stays in mzLib (your D1), and SQL does only equality joins, as you proposed.

**3. The join: definition text plus the test.**
- The catalog's SQL is ours to write and maintain.
- Please send:
  - the rules in `DEFINITIONS.md`;
  - the fixture inputs and the expected group rows (today's `GoGroupAnnotator` output on mzLib's fixtures) as files.
- We commit those as a fixture and run the join against them in CI. That way your test and ours are the same rows.

## GO-D12 (answer): all three aspects, direct only

- **Keep BP and MF.** Enrichment of age effects is the likeliest high-impact use, and it reads BP and MF.
- **To keep (a) small, store each accession's direct annotations only:**
  - (a): accession, go_id, aspect, evidence, inherited (D37), and one term-less row per entry with no terms.
- **Add the ancestor closure, once per go.obo:** (term, ancestor) under go's own propagation rule. It is either a
  part of (b) or a fourth artefact keyed by go.obo sha256 alone.
  - The view propagates with one equality join to the closure.
  - A propagated term's evidence is the union over the direct terms below it (your D8): a `string_agg(DISTINCT ...)`
    in the view.
  - Propagation stays go's (the closure is go's output), and no ontology walk sits in SQL.
- **What it saves:** we have not measured it. Today's per-deposit file was 439,620 group x term rows on one rat deposit,
  ancestors included. The rat XML carries 260,821 GO references in all. We will measure both shapes on the three
  species' databases when you have a branch, or before then if you would like the number to decide.
- **If the closure cannot reproduce v1's rows exactly** (relation types, D37 inheritance through an ancestor), tell us,
  and (a) with propagated rows is our fallback.
