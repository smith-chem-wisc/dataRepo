## 1. GO-E1: does D36 stop the target's GO reaching entrapment members?

**No.** D36 only adds the `entrapment_members` column. It does not change which terms a member
gets. Checked against `GoGroupAnnotator` as released in 1.0.593 (`smith/master` `91ee11c3c`):

1. **An entrapment member that is not in the annotation database gets nothing.** Inheritance runs
   only on UniProt-grammar accessions, and `Random_P04406_f1` is not one. So the member reads
   `no_entry`. It never borrows its target's GO.
2. **An entrapment member that IS in the annotation database is annotated from its own entry**,
   like any other protein. That is how the leak gets through. If the database was built by a #1271
   generator from before `c3400f00`, each permuted partner (`Random_<acc>_f<n>`) carries its target's
   `dbReference`s, so those terms land on the row with the partner listed in `accession_used`.
   The fix belongs in the generator, not in go. The entrapment project made it in their 002: **#1271
   at `c3400f00` gives permuted partners no references.** **#1271 is still OPEN**, so no released
   mzLib contains it.
3. **A check that tells the two cases apart, from your file alone:** a permuted partner
   (`Random_<acc>_f<n>`) should never appear in `accession_used`. If one does, the database was
   built before the fix. A foreign-arm member (`Random_foreign_<acc>`) in `accession_used` is
   **correct**. Its references are true of its own sequence, so the generator keeps them on purpose
   (entrapment 002, GO-E6). Do not refuse those.

**GO-D2 (request):** keep your entrapment refusal until #1271 merges. After that, you may replace it
with the check in §1.3 if you want to: refuse a file where any `Random_<acc>_f<n>` appears in
`accession_used`, and accept the rest. Tell us which one you choose. go will post here when #1271
merges.

## 2. Charter v0.6

The OWNS and DELIVERS cells read correctly. We checked that #1353, #1365 and #1366 are all in
the `1.0.593` tag. One change:

**GO-D3 (request):** the decision range is **go D1-D38**, not D1-D36 (lines 25 and 238). Both new
decisions merged in #1366 and are in 1.0.593:

- **D37:** an isoform that is in the annotation database but has no GO of its own takes its
  entry's terms, flagged `inherited`. UniProt puts GO on the entry, not on its isoforms. **This
  changes your `inherited` column:** isoforms in a database searched with isoforms as separate
  entries now arrive `inherited = true`, where before D37 they read `no_go_terms`.
- **D38:** a group that MetaMorpheus wrote twice is annotated once, so its rows appear once.
