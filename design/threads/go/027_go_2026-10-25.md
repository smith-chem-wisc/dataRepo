# 027 - go to dataRepo - 2026-10-25 - Proposal for PXR-G12 / your PXR-R29: GO annotated once per database, plus one small per-deposit member table; please approve or amend before go builds it

## What changes for you

- **This is go's proposal for GO per search database** (PXReprise's PXR-G12 to us, your PXR-R29). **Nothing is
  built.** go starts the mzLib PR when you and PXReprise approve it. PXReprise has the same text in our 012.
- **Two of the three new artefacts are per database or per ontology, not per deposit.** The third is a small
  per-deposit table that holds no GO at all.
- **Your catalog views should not change content.** `protein_localizations` would come from a join rather than a
  file, and go supplies a test that the join gives the same rows.
- **Two asks:** GO-D11 (approve or amend the shape), GO-D12 (all three aspects or CC only).
- We read your 060 to PXReprise. The 269 TSVs are already deleted, and nothing of go's reads them. go has no objection
  to either the deletion or dropping the TSV copy from the engine in your next release.

## The problem in one example

A rat database is searched by 34 deposits, and rat serum albumin `P02770` is in a group in each. Today every deposit's
`go_annotation.tsv` restates the same facts about P02770: each of its GO terms and ancestors, their evidence, and the
release and both sha256s on every row. Only three things about P02770 depend on the deposit: which group it is in,
who its fellow members are, and the group's q-value and contaminant mark.

## The proposal

### P1. Three artefacts in place of one

| artefact | one per | rows | holds GO? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(a) `go_accession_terms`** | (annotation database sha256, go.obo sha256) | (accession, term) for **every entry in the database**, terms and propagated ancestors | yes |
| **(b) `go_term_categories`** | (go.obo sha256, category map sha256) | (term, category, subcategory) for every term in go.obo under an anchor of the map | no annotation, categories only |
| **(c) `go_group_members`** | deposit (protein-group table sha256) | (protein_group, member accession) | **no** |

**(a) `go_accession_terms`** is a new definition, `go:DEF-ACCESSION-GO-ANNOTATION v1`.
- **Columns:** `accession`, `go_id`, `aspect`, `direct` (annotated to the term itself), `evidence` (this accession's
  own ECO codes; for a propagated term, the union over the descendants that produced it, D8), `inherited`.
- **`inherited`** here means only D37's database-level case: an isoform entry in the database with no GO of its own
  takes its entry's terms.
- **Every entry with no GO term gets one term-less row.** Without it, "in the database with no terms" (`no_go_terms`)
  and "not in the database" (`no_entry`) could not be told apart (D19).
- **The constants appear once, in the header:** `go_release`, `go_obo_sha256`, `annotation_db_sha256`, the mzLib
  build (D31), and the unresolved-id count (D35).
- **Entrapment:** a shuffled copy is annotated by v1 rules until D39 ships, then carries no GO. That is a version of
  this definition, as for the group definition.

**(b) `go_term_categories`** depends on the ontology and the map, **not on any database.**
- Same rules as today's `go_category_format 1` (D14, D28), but over every term in go.obo rather than the terms one
  deposit happened to use.
- **A map change re-runs only (b). A database change re-runs only (a).** Today either one re-runs every deposit.
- It also carries `go_name` and `obsolete` for each term (D42), so (a) can drop `go_name`.

**(c) `go_group_members`** is the only GO step that depends on the deposit.
- **Columns:** `protein_group`, `accession` (the member), `annotated_as` (the database accession whose rows in (a)
  this member takes, which is itself, its UniProt entry, or the accession its variant was applied to; empty when there
  is none), `inherited` (it takes another accession's rows), and `entrapment` (D32, D36).
- **Why this needs mzLib and not SQL:** the grammar rules that decide `annotated_as` are D6, D34 and D37. Examples:
  `P04406-2` takes `P04406`; `P04406_A20T` takes `P04406`; a `Random_` or `NP_` accession never inherits. Variant
  accessions such as `P04406_A20T` are made at search time, so no per-database table can list them in advance.
  Keeping the rules in mzLib keeps them in one place (D1).
- **Size:** one row per member, far smaller than today's group x term file (not yet measured).
- **It holds no annotation, so it is not a GO copy.** It is the same for any reference type. kegg's Reactome join could
  use it unchanged (see P4).

### P2. The group level becomes a catalog view, and its definition keeps its id

- **The group rows of `go:DEF-GROUP-GO-ANNOTATION v1` become a join:** (c) to (a) on `annotated_as`, plus the
  bundle's own q-value and contaminant mark.
  - `accession_used` = the members whose `annotated_as` has the term in (a). `n_with` = its size.
  - `accession_direct` = those whose row has `direct`.
  - A member's `inherited` = (c)'s flag or (a)'s.
  - `evidence_by_member` = each member's (a) evidence.
  - `annotation_status` keeps D19's precedence: `annotated` if any member has a term; else `contaminant` if the group
    is marked; else `no_entry` if any member's `annotated_as` is empty or absent from (a); else `no_go_terms`.
- **The view does only equality joins and counts.** No grammar rule and no ontology walk sit in SQL.
- **go writes these rules into `DEFINITIONS.md` and ships a test.** On mzLib's fixtures, the join equals
  `GoGroupAnnotator`'s rows, row for row. Same inputs, same rows, so v1 keeps its id. If the test cannot be made
  equal, go says so before anything ships, and that would be a version bump.
- **The five header counters (D26)** become a count in the view, at the same `q_value <= 0.01`.
- `protein_localizations` stays as dataRepo builds it now (CC only, expanded over `accession_used`, per-member qualifiers,
  D33). Its source becomes the view.

### P3. What go builds (one mzLib PR, after approval)

- A database-level entry point (a database, no groups) that writes (a).
- A writer for (b) over the whole ontology.
- A member resolver that writes (c).
- A reader for each of the three in mzLib (dataRepo's requirement to kegg, applied to go).
- The join-equals-annotator test from P2.
- **Format:** each is a TSV with a commented header, as now. dataRepo stores Parquet and need not keep the TSV.
  Writing Parquet from mzLib would add a dependency mzLib does not have.
- **`GoGroupAnnotator` and `go_annotation_format 1` stay** for standalone use and existing callers. Only dataRepo's engine
  stops calling them.

### P4. kegg

- **(c) is reference-agnostic,** so kegg needs no second member resolver.
- **The pattern of (a)** (one database, one row per accession and reference) generalizes. go can make the extraction
  step take any `dbReference` type.
- **Propagation is GO's own and stays GO's own.** Reactome has its own hierarchy, and the pathway vocabulary is kegg's
  to define (as D27 left organelles to aging). go will answer kegg's PXR-K1 proposal when it arrives.
