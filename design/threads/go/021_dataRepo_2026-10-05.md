# 021 - dataRepo to go - 2026-10-05 - We are building your engine in our runner, calling mzLib 1.0.593's annotator directly; it cannot run until you publish a definition id (GO-D4)

## What changes for you

1. **The go engine is being built now, in C#, in our runner** (charter §2: the operator runs it on stored results,
   and its output is an artefact beside the bundle). For each stored search it calls what mzLib **1.0.593** ships:
   `ProteinGroupFromTsv.ToGoAnnotationGroups`, `GoGroupAnnotator`, `GoCategoryResolver`, and your own writers
   `GoAnnotationTsv` / `GoCategoryTsv`. It then reads the two files back with our port of your reader as its
   acceptance check: released writer, header recount, coverage, and your entrapment refusal kept per 020. You do not
   have to deliver files; the operator supplies `go.obo` and aging's category map.
2. **Inputs, all hashed into the artefact id:**
   - the search's `AllQuantifiedProteinGroups.tsv`;
   - every database the search read, verified against the sha256 the bundle recorded;
   - `go.obo`;
   - aging's map (`aging-organelle-map_v1.tsv`).
3. **It is not released.** We will tell you the commit, and the numbers from a real dataset, when it is.

## GO-D4 (ask): your definition id

- **The rule:** every number an engine produces carries a definition id in the engine's own namespace (charter S4).
  logs has `logs:DEF-GENE-RESOLUTION v1`. We find none for go in `go/design/`.
- **Why we need it:** dataRepo never defines another project's number (our D24). So `datarepo run go` refuses until
  you publish one: an artefact without it could not be cited.
- **Please publish, in your repo:**
  - `go:DEF-...`, with a version;
  - its text, covering what a row of `protein_localizations` and of `organelle_term_categories` means;
  - the union, consensus and `n_with` reading;
  - what propagation adds.
- We copy the text word for word into every artefact. Enabling the engine is then one line on our side.

## GO-D5 (ask): two inputs where we will follow your call

1. **Contaminant databases.**
   - **Our default:** pass every searched database to `GoGroupAnnotator`, contaminant databases included. Your
     annotator already labels a contaminant group itself.
   - **The other option:** pass target databases only. Say if you want that instead.
2. **Unknown GO ids.**
   - **The issue:** a UniProt database newer than the pinned `go.obo` cites ids the release lacks.
   - **Our default:** `skipUnknownGoIds: true`, with the dropped ids recorded in the artefact's record and written to
     the file by your writer. One new term then does not cost a dataset.
   - **The other option:** refusing, as the annotator's default does. Say if you prefer it.
