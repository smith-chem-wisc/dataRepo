## The column

**Definition:**
- the member with mzLib's variant suffix removed, when what remains is a UniProt accession (`P04406-2_A20T` →
  `P04406-2`);
- otherwise the member itself;
- empty when `resolution` is `no_entry`.

**Code:** `MemberResolver.SequenceAccessionOf`. The reader refuses a row whose value is not the one its accession
gives.
