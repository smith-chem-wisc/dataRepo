## GO-D7 (ask): derive the per-accession qualifiers from the per-member columns

**The issue.** Your engine expands go's (group, term) row over `accession_used`, which is correct
(D22). But each expanded row copies three columns that go writes as group-level summaries pooled over
the carrying members (D33). They are not facts about any single accession:

| go column | what it means on go's row | what it becomes on your per-accession row |
|---|---|---|
| `propagated` | true only when **no** carrying member cites the term itself | `false` for a member that holds the term only through a descendant, whenever another member cites it |
| `inherited` | true only when **every** carrying member inherited it | `false` for an isoform that inherited the term, whenever another member carries it from its own entry |
| `evidence` | every ECO code from any carrying member | one member's IDA is attached to another member's row |

**Example.** Group `P1|P2`, term *mitochondrion*:
- P1 cites it directly (IDA).
- P2 cites only *mitochondrial matrix* (IEA).
- go's row: `accession_used P1;P2`, `accession_direct P1`, `propagated false`, `evidence ECO-IDA;ECO-IEA`.
- Your P2 row then reads "directly annotated, with IDA evidence". Neither is true of P2.

The `inherited` case matters most. aging builds organelle claims on `inherited = false` (go 020 §3,
GO-A5), and on mixed rows that flag currently undercounts inheritance. Only multi-member groups are
affected, about 2 % of groups, but those are exactly the rows these columns exist for.

**What we want.** For each accession `a` expanded from a go row, set:
- `inherited` = `a` ∈ `accession_inherited`;
- `propagated` = `a` ∉ `accession_direct`;
- `evidence` = `evidence_by_member[a]` (the codes after `a=`, `,`-separated).

`n_with`, `n_members`, `q_value` and `protein_group` stay group-level, as they are now. This needs no
schema change: you already read all three per-member columns (your Go.cs remarks), and the
definition's "The two dataRepo tables" section states the rule. Please tell us the commit when it
lands. Until then, rows from multi-member groups are not v1 rows.
