## What changes for you

1. **Charter v0.4 (`874bf61`, carried into v0.5):** your four merged cells read right; change nothing
   in them. One cell is now short because of item 2: the go row's OWNS cell lists the per-member
   evidence as "(`n_members`, `n_with`, `accession_used`, `propagated`, `inherited`)". Please make it
   "(`n_members`, `n_with`, `accession_used`, `accession_direct`, `accession_inherited`,
   `evidence_by_member`, `propagated`, `inherited`)" and the decision range "go D1-D35".

2. **Three new columns on every annotation row (D33).** They come from pcruzparri's review of
   mzLib#1353, mzLib `341171d4`, 2026-09-28. The full column order is now:
   `protein_group, accession_used, accession_direct, accession_inherited, go_id, go_name, aspect,
   evidence, evidence_by_member, inherited, propagated, n_members, n_with, annotation_status, q_value,
   go_release, go_obo_sha256, annotation_db_sha256`.
   - `accession_direct` and `accession_inherited` are `;`-joined member sets, like `accession_used`,
     and are empty on a term-less row.
   - `evidence_by_member` is `member=code,code;member=code`, members in ordinal order. A value
     containing `;`, `=` or `,` is refused at write time.
   - `evidence`, `propagated` and `inherited` stay as **group-level summaries**. They cannot be
     combined with `n_with`; the new columns are the per-member versions.

   `go_annotation_format` stays `1`, because nothing has been released. Your schema 0.0.10 columns are
   all unchanged in meaning. Whether to store the new three is your call.

3. **`inherited` now also covers sequence variants (D34).** Alexander-Sol's review, mzLib `364c0165`.
   A member named the way mzLib names an applied variant (`P04406_A20T`) and absent from the annotation
   XML takes the terms of the accession it was applied to, or else its UniProt entry. It is flagged
   `inherited` and listed in `accession_inherited`, exactly as an isoform is (D6). Such groups used to
   read `no_entry` and wrote no stored row. They now read `annotated`, so your not-stored tally falls
   by the same number. Entrapment partners (`Random_...`) and RefSeq `NP_...` never take this path.

4. **An optional header line, `#!unresolved_go_ids N` (D35).** Alexander-Sol's review, mzLib
   `d9854554`. Strict is still the default: a GO id missing from the pinned go.obo stops the run. A
   caller may opt to skip such ids instead, and then the file says how many were skipped. The line is
   written **only** when the count is above zero, so a strict run's file is byte-identical to before.
   It sits with the provenance lines, after `source_file_sha256` and before `counter_q_value_max`. It
   is not a sixth counter: D26's five stand.
