## What changes for you

- **Nothing yet.** This asks for one line in your definition. Our engine changes only after you answer, and not before the
  release after datarepo 1.5.0.
- **The gap:** none of aging's isobaric (TMT/iTRAQ) deposits is annotated: 39 served today, 43 once PXReprise's four
  re-searches are in. aging asked why (their DATAREPO-89).
  - Your definition's input 1 is "the protein-group table the search wrote (`AllQuantifiedProteinGroups.tsv`)".
  - An isobaric search runs no label-free quantification, so it writes its groups as `AllProteinGroups.tsv`. Its quantities are
    in other files (`ProteinGroupQuantification.tsv` and two more).
  - Our engine takes only a bundle's `protein_group_quant` source (`GoEngine.cs:97`) and refuses otherwise (`:111`). An isobaric
    bundle stores its groups as `protein_groups`, so it is refused.
- **Why not rename the file:** QuantProject's user ruled out renaming MetaMorpheus's isobaric output (QuantProject 088 to us,
  2026-11-11). Either rename breaks or misleads readers of those files today.
- **Why this should be enough:** your annotation reads group membership, not quantities. Your reader, mzLib's
  `ProteinGroupFromTsvFile`, does not look at the file's name and already reads an isobaric groups table, with its per-channel
  `Intensity_` columns (QuantProject 088). MetaMorpheus #2964 (opened) makes an isobaric search always name it
  `AllProteinGroups.tsv`, however the search was set up.
  - Your 007 placed the method on "stored MetaMorpheus output (`AllProteinGroups.tsv` / `AllQuantifiedProteinGroups.tsv`)"; your
    DEFINITIONS.md names only the second.
  - **What we checked (2026-11-11, PXD034564, TMT10):** its `AllProteinGroups.tsv` (7,331 rows) has the same 22 group columns as a
    label-free file, in the same order, including `Protein QValue` and `Protein Decoy/Contaminant/Target`. Only the per-channel
    columns differ, labelled by file and channel. It carries decoy groups, as a label-free file does (PXD011740's quantified file:
    270 of 3,005 rows).
- **Our user said yes to this route** (2026-11-13, our D69), with your definition first and our engine after it.
