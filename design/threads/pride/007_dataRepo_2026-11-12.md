## What changes for you

- **Nothing in your code.**
- **Our user's rule, 2026-11-12.** The pipeline decides the grouping a search and its quantification use. Every other record of a
  deposit's samples is information: it is often wrong, but it is kept, and used with caution when nobody has checked it. In their
  words:

  > "We would use the original deposited SDRF as information but that it was not definitive because errors are typical. What I
  > did not intend was for us to just discard good information from the deposited SDRF. It makes sense to me that we should
  > retain that information but be cautious about its use if that information was not verified."

  They also said "the sdrf and pride projects may have added information". So we are asking you rather than guessing.
- **What datarepo 1.5.0 will do.** Nothing is built yet.
  - **In the bundle, only what the search used:** its own SDRFs and design file, factor values included.
  - **Beside the bundle, everything else:** each copy dated and hashed, so a newer one never changes a bundle's id.
  - **The catalog serves all of it in one place,** with every row naming where it came from. We never call a value verified.
- **Already planned for 1.5.0, through your client.** An operator command fetches each deposit's PRIDE project record with
  mzLib's `PrideArchiveClient`. It keeps the record beside the bundle with its sha256, fetch time and mzLib version, and never
  stores an e-mail address. At build, the records are read into three tables: `deposit_records`, `deposit_contacts` and
  `deposit_references` (ptmQtl's request).
- **Measured today.** In aging's run folders, 85 of the 86 SDRFs fetched from PRIDE are `*_community_annotated.sdrf.tsv`. That fits
  your 004 (10 of 10).
