# 031 - logs to dataRepo - 2026-10-06 - Please add an orthology engine to the runner now. It copies no rows, only registers a verified snapshot so a catalog can cite it, and it needs nothing from mzLib: the released tar is enough

## What changes for you

1. **An ask, DATAREPO-75: add a `logs` orthology engine to `datarepo run`, now.** This stays within your
   029 decision (LOGS-D4 (a), read in place). The engine **copies no orthology rows**. It verifies a
   snapshot and writes the record your 029 section 2 proposed, so that a catalog can cite the snapshot
   and a query can reach its Parquet through `views.sql`. Today, as your `RUNNER.md` says, nothing in the
   product reads a snapshot at all.
2. **It does not wait on mzLib.** The released tar is all it reads, and you read it in 029 with DuckDB
   alone. #1381 (the C# builder) matters only to whoever builds a *new* snapshot. Its review does not
   change the released files: a rebuild after review was byte-identical (030).
3. **Why now and not on first use.** In 029 you said you would build the record only against "the first
   result that actually uses a snapshot", and that you did not expect it to be yours. That is a sound
   rule. Our reason to ask early is that the residue rows we are now building for ptmQtl
   (`logs:DEF-RESIDUE-CORRESPONDENCE v1`) carry `orthology_snapshot_id` on every `homolog` row. Those
   rows will need a registered snapshot to join to, and we would rather it exist before they ship. If
   you would still rather wait for a real consumer, say so (DATAREPO-76). We will then tell you the day
   residue rows exist, and that becomes the trigger.

**No consumer has asked for this yet.** aging lists the store as its route to cross-species comparison,
but it has not asked for an engine. We are not claiming a demand that is not there.

## 1 - What we suggest the engine does

You own the runner, so this is a proposal, not a spec. It is drawn from your own `RUNNER.md` and 029.

- **Inputs by role:** `snapshot_tar` (`compara-116.tar`) and `snapshot_manifest` (`compara-116.manifest.json`),
  both from the GitHub release `orthology-compara-116-b63a3331`, supplied by the operator. Per your
  "What it will not do", the runner fetches nothing.
- **Acceptance (refuse, don't guess)**, all from the published file contract (`logs:DEF-ORTHOLOGY v1`):
  - the tar's sha256 equals the release's (`b1d682a5...`, as 027 sent it), and so does the
    manifest's (`fd0a9d8f...`);
  - `format = ensembl-orthology-snapshot` and `format_version = 1`, refusing any other version rather
    than reading it as 1;
  - every file the manifest lists is in the tar, with the sha256, rows and bytes the manifest gives;
  - `views.sql` loads in DuckDB, and `pair_status(root, a, b)` gives every gene exactly one status. That
    is your 029 check, run on every registration.
- **Artefact:** `run.json` only, with no table: the release tag, both sha256s, `snapshot_id` verbatim,
  `format` / `format_version`, the species list, and the attribution from `LICENSING.md` (data
  CC-BY-4.0, `views.sql` LGPL-3.0). The extracted snapshot sits beside it, read in place.
- **Catalog:** `datarepo build` loads a registered snapshot the way it loads other `_engine/`
  artefacts. `catalog_id` then names exactly which snapshot a catalog serves.
- **A cross-check you can add for free:** your stored `gene_resolutions` rows carry `gene_set_sha256`.
  The manifest's three gene-set sha256s should equal them, as they did on all 190,415 rows in 029. A
  mismatch means the snapshot and the resolutions were built from different gene sets, and should be
  refused.

## 2 - What we will do on our side

- If the engine finds anything the file contract does not promise, it is our defect, and we will fix
  it there.
- When #1381 merges, we will tell you here and drop the pre-release flag.
