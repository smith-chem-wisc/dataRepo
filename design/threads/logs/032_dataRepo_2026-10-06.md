## What it does

```
datarepo run logs.register_orthology --store <store> \
  --input snapshot_tar=compara-116.tar --input snapshot_manifest=compara-116.manifest.json
```

It takes no accession: one snapshot per store.

**Acceptance.** Every item refuses the run:
- **Your release, by sha256.** The tar must be `b1d682a5…` and the manifest `fd0a9d8f…`.
  - The list of your releases is `OrthologyEngine.Releases`, copied from 027/030/031. A tar not on it is refused.
  - A new snapshot is one line there and a datarepo release.
- **The manifest.** The tar's `manifest.json` must be byte-identical to the manifest given.
- **The format.** `format = ensembl-orthology-snapshot` and `format_version = 1`. Any other version is refused,
  never read as 1.
- **The files.** The tar must hold exactly the files the manifest lists, plus `manifest.json`. Each must match its
  sha256 and bytes, and each Parquet file its rows. The tar must have one top-level directory and regular files
  only.
- **`views.sql`.** It must load in DuckDB. For every ordered pair of species, `pair_status` must give every gene of
  the first exactly one of its four statuses.

**What it writes.** The artefact is `run.json` plus the extracted snapshot under `snapshot/`. It copies no rows. A
query loads `snapshot/views.sql` and passes `<artefact>/snapshot` as `root`.

`run.json` records, under `snapshot`:
- the tag and `snapshot_id` verbatim (your 030 point 2);
- `format` and `format_version`, the source, release and species;
- the three gene-set sha256s;
- your `LICENSING.md` attribution: data CC-BY-4.0, `views.sql` LGPL-3.0 (mzLib).

The definition id is `logs:DEF-ORTHOLOGY v1`.

**The catalog.** `build` cites the registered snapshot in `catalog_engine_artefacts` and in the catalog id, so a
catalog names exactly the snapshot it serves. Two registered snapshots are refused rather than one picked.

**Your cross-check (031 section 1) runs at build.** If any loaded `gene_resolutions` row's `gene_set_sha256` is
not one of the snapshot's three, the build fails and names the gene set.
- We moved it from registration because that is where both are loaded together.
- At registration, the gene resolutions can still change: aging re-runs `logs.resolve_genes` during its switch.
