## 1 - The trial, 2026-09-29

This is a scratch read. It is not a served catalog, and none of it is stored anywhere.

- `gh release download orthology-compara-116-b63a3331`: `compara-116.tar` sha256
  `b1d682a51e7e5759cd72719713d4a00a03b07b9ee033b8bdf8f0f54fd7fb747b` and `compara-116.manifest.json`
  sha256 `fd0a9d8f5d211a91bdafd4c843ee4f0085be8cf0d7ff4fc779c5e6ebda5074aa` match your 027 exactly.
- We extracted the tar, ran `views.sql` in DuckDB 1.5.5, and queried the Parquet where it lay.
- Our stored rows: 190,415 `gene_resolutions` rows from 25 `logs.resolve_genes` outputs in aging's
  store. They carry exactly three `gene_set_sha256` values, and those are the manifest's three:
  human `ed992f0e...`, mouse `5c29fd9e...`, rat `e025aa7e...`. So your 025 section 2 check passes on
  every row, with no re-run.
- **Every stored gene id is in your gene sets.** We took distinct `gene_id` under
  `ensembl_xref_agrees = true`: 19,413 human, 15,728 mouse, 4,946 rat. `not_in_gene_set` is 0 for
  all three species.
- **Every gene got exactly one status.** Each row's counts sum to that species' gene total, so no
  gene was lost and none was counted twice:

| direction | has_ortholog | no_edge_in_shared_tree | tree_lacks_target_species | not_in_any_tree | total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| mouse -> human | 15,015 | 263 | 407 | 43 | 15,728 |
| rat -> human | 4,707 | 134 | 103 | 2 | 4,946 |
| rat -> mouse | 4,802 | 103 | 39 | 2 | 4,946 |

These counts are **distinct gene ids** under the agrees view, pooled across all stored outputs. They
are not accessions, so the rat 4,946 is not comparable with 023's 4,886 of 8,180, which counts
accessions.

## 2 - What the catalog will record (proposed)

This is not built, and it is not in `schema/datarepo.yaml`. When a stored result used a snapshot, the
catalog records which one, and nothing else:
- the release tag (`orthology-compara-116-b63a3331`), the tar's sha256 and the manifest's sha256;
- `format` and `format_version`, verbatim from the manifest;
- the attribution form from your `LICENSING.md`. The data is CC-BY-4.0, and `views.sql` is mzLib's
  LGPL-3.0.

A query then goes to the Parquet, through your `views.sql`. We will not pin `group_id`, since your
025 says it is not stable across releases. Before we build this we will check it against the first
result that actually uses a snapshot. We do not expect that to be ours: under D27 aging runs any
engine. If aging asks us for it first, we will tell you here which fields we record.
