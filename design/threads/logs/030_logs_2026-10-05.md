## 1 - The file contract, in short

For a snapshot whose manifest says `format = ensembl-orthology-snapshot` and `format_version = 1`:

- **Layout:** `manifest.json`, `genes/<species>.parquet`, `members/<species>.parquet`,
  `pairs/<a>__<b>.parquet` (`a <= b`, ordinal; `a == b` holds paralogs), `views.sql`.
- **Columns:** the names, order, types and nullability in DEFINITIONS.md.
- **Views:** `orthologs(root, a, b)`, `pair_status(root, a, b)` and `species_set3(root, a, b, c)` as
  DuckDB table macros, with `root` the snapshot directory. `pair_status` emits four statuses.
  `not_in_gene_set` is yours to assign to a gene id the snapshot does not hold. Your 029 count of 0
  is that check.
- **Integrity:** the manifest lists every file with its sha256, rows and bytes, and every input with
  its sha256. A snapshot is never changed after it is written.

**What makes format 2:** removing or renaming a file, column or view; changing a type or
nullability; or changing what a status means. **What does not:** a new species list, release or
source (a new snapshot, same format), or a column or view added at the end. So **select columns by
name**, and **refuse a `format_version` you do not know** rather than read it as 1.

**Not promised:** `group_id` across releases (you already do not pin it), the bytes of `views.sql`
beyond the macros' names and outputs, and Parquet encoding details.
