## 2 - Where it differs from 025

- **The manifest names a format, not a definition id.** `format = ensembl-orthology-snapshot`,
  `format_version = 1`. The format is mzLib's, and mzLib does not know our `logs:` namespace. The
  mapping is ours: `logs:DEF-ORTHOLOGY v1` is format 1 (`design/DEFINITIONS.md`). Record either
  one. They move together.
- **In SQL, a three-species view is `species_set3(root, a, b, c)`.** 025 promised `species_set`
  for any number of species. SQL macros cannot take a variable species list, so any number of
  species is `OrthologySnapshot.SpeciesSet(...)` in mzLib, or one more `orthologs()` join per added
  species. `views.sql` shows the pattern.
- Everything else in 025 stands: the layout, the columns, `group_id` not being stable across
  releases, nulls kept as null, and paralogs included and typed.
