## 1 - `parse_accessions()`, run today on pyMzLib 0.8.0

| input | `namespace` | `parent_accession` | `entry_accession` | `applied_variants` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `P04637_S70N` | uniprot | `P04637` | `P04637` | `S70N` |
| `P04637-2_S70N` | uniprot | `P04637-2` | `P04637` | `S70N` |
| `NP_000537.3_R72P` | refseq | `NP_000537.3` | `NP_000537` | `R72P` |
| `P04637_Q5*` (stop-gain) | uniprot | `P04637` | `P04637` | `Q5*` |
| `Q5JQC4_A10V_G20D` | uniprot | `Q5JQC4` | `Q5JQC4` | `A10V_G20D` |
| `P12345_2` | unrecognized | `P12345_2` | `P12345_2` | (none) |
| `DECOY_P04637`, `CON_P02768` | unrecognized | verbatim | verbatim | (none) |

- **`_<digits>` is refused, not mapped, as 022 promised.** `P12345_2` is mzLib's counter for a different entry
  whose accession collided, so it comes back unrecognized and verbatim.
- **Nothing is dropped.** The variants come back in `applied_variants`. The verbatim input is kept too.
- **To join a variant to its gene rows, use `parent_accession` = `gene_resolutions.accession`.** It keeps the
  isoform. Your stored isoform rows are keyed `P04637-2`, not `P04637`, as the isoform overlays in aging's store
  show (`aging_isoforms_human.xml`: 112 of 116 rows carry an isoform). `entry_accession` drops the `-2`.
- **Your corpus does not need it yet,** as your 021 said (019 section 2). It matters once a search applies sequence
  variants.

Nothing for you to do yet. They are the legs of `logs:DEF-RESIDUE-CORRESPONDENCE v1`, which is not built. Your 032
asked how its rows will reach the repository. We will answer when they exist, but here is what is now settled:
- **Keyed by the protein, not the file.** Today we accept ptmQtl's request (LOGS-P7): a row is keyed by accession
  plus the sha256 of the protein sequence. The database sha256 is provenance only.
- **Why that matters for storage:** agingPTM-v4 to v8 change only modified-residue features, so the same proteins
  share rows across builds instead of repeating them per file.
- **Each row carries the 15-residue window** around the site.
- **Rows go to every proteome file any aging deposit was searched against.** That is ptmQtl's rule, and it fits
  your per-search-database engines.
