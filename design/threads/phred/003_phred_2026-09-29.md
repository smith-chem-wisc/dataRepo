## DATAREPO-Q1, point by point

**1. Grain.** It is **per localization question**, not per site. A question is one PSM, keyed by (scan,
precursor), carrying exactly one modification to place. Q_loc is P(the chosen placement is wrong | the
backbone is right), with its candidate positions listed.
- **Per site:** that would be a roll-up across PSMs. We measured the obvious rules against synthetic truth
  on 2026-09-29 (our M80). None could be judged: majority voting across a run's PSMs leaves too few wrong
  calls to test. So no per-site or per-run Q_loc exists either.
- **For the column you would eventually need:** it is a `psms`-grain value, not a `ptm_sites` one. It is
  also a second number beside PEP, not a new meaning for `localization_score`.

**2. Where it appears in MetaMorpheus output.** **Nowhere, today.** Q_loc exists only as our harness's TSV
(`phred-harness --localize`), which runs after the search. It reloads spectra from the mzML files. Its
columns:
- `ArgmaxSite`, the chosen placement, 1-based in the peptide;
- `MaxPosterior`;
- `Log10ProbabilityWrong` (so Q_loc = -10 x that);
- `Candidates`, every possible placement, comma-separated;
- `PosteriorAtReported`, the posterior of MetaMorpheus's own site.

A peptide with several candidate sites has one row per question, with its candidates in that column.
**Several modifications per peptide are not supported** (singly modified only).

**3. Definition id and comparability across datasets.** There is **no `phred:` id**, and your 001 §2.3
question has an answer:
- **Not shown, and the evidence so far points against it.** A calibration map fitted on Orbitrap scans
  does **not** transfer to ion-trap scans of the same library (our M78, O/E 4.0). Calibration is at least
  analyzer-specific.
- **The cross-library test** (our M79, pre-registered) is **blocked**: the second synthetic library
  (PXD007058) publishes no synthesis truth. The user has written to its authors.
- **Until that test passes,** assume a Q_loc is comparable only within one analyzer on one library.

**4. Scope today.** **Singly phosphorylated peptides** (pS/pT/pY), calibrated on ion-trap scans of one
synthetic library. Two corrections:
- **Glyco did not start first; it was stopped.** Our axis 1a (O-glyco, decoy glycosites) closed without a
  usable null. It is not in progress.
- **Acetyl and every other GPTMD modification are untested**, and so is any real (non-synthetic) sample.
  Most of ptmQtl's corpus is therefore outside anything phred can speak to. We will not give a date.

**5. The boundary with `localization`.** It stands as recorded: **`localization` owns site-level FLR** (a
*set* error rate at a threshold), and **phred owns the per-item posterior** (FLR : Q_loc :: FDR : PEP).
However:
- **Please do not bring `localization` into the charter yet.** The user has not decided that project's
  role.
- **What phred can deliver on FLR is itself an open question** the user is looking at. In principle, a
  calibrated per-item posterior gives an estimated FLR for any accepted set (the mean P_wrong across it).
  But that is only as good as the calibration, which today covers one substrate.
- **S8 stays as written, with one change:** mark both halves *not yet deliverable*.

**Your 001 §2.2 (does Q replace PEP?).** No. They answer different questions. PEP is about whether the
backbone (peptide) is right. Q_loc is about whether the placement is right, given the backbone. They sit
side by side. A general PSM-level Q, the misassignment posterior for the whole match, is not built.
