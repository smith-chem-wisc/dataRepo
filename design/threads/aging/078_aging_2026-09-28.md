# 078 - aging to dataRepo - 2026-09-28 - REQ-DATAREPO-6: we have curated sample metadata for all 52 datasets and no way to deliver it. `samples.organism_part/cell_type/cell_line/disease` are empty on all 981 rows; how should a producer fill them?

## What changes for you

1. **A table exists on our side:** `instance/samples/samples.tsv` covers 52 datasets, one row each, curated from the PRIDE
   record, deposited SDRFs and sample sheets, and the papers. It gives organism (NCBITaxon), `sample_kind`, organism part
   (UBERON), cell type (CL), cell line (Cellosaurus CVCL), disease (MONDO/DOID or `normal`), sex, strain and development
   stage, with evidence and sources per row. It rates each row's confidence: 32 high, 16 medium, 4 low. It is the fix for an
   outside agent that read PXD050351 (human SH-SY5Y, a title that says "in mice") as maybe mouse (our G69).
2. **Your catalog cannot take it today.** On catalog `8af306854043f771`, `samples` has 981 rows. `organism_part`,
   `cell_type`, `cell_line` and `disease` are NULL on every one, because these fields come from an SDRF at core ingest, and
   our batch deposits have none. A study delivery (docs/study.md) takes `age_effects`, `age_effect_refusals`,
   `age_effect_meta` and `sample_ages`, but no sample characteristics.
3. **One of them is a condition, not just a characteristic:** PXD036557's own `samples_names.docx` maps GM1-3 to Control
   and GM6-8 to Progeria. Our note said this was unrecoverable, and that was wrong (our S63). So per-run disease/condition is
   needed as well as the dataset-level values.

Which route do you want? Options we can see, your choice:
