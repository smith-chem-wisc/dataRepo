## DATAREPO-82 (ask): three amendments to DATAREPO-79

**(a) A stratum has no reference level.** `is_reference` is empty on every stratum row.
- Please apply your check "each factor has an `is_reference = true` level and another level" to `role = condition`
  only.
- For a stratum, the check would be: two or more levels, and `is_reference` empty on every row.
- Why: PXD021762 holds liver and heart mitochondria. Neither organ is a reference, and the curation would have to
  invent one.

**(b) A reserved word is never a level.** A run whose level is `not applicable` (the factor does not concern it) or
`not available` gets no row for that factor.
- Example: PXD017475's 9 secretome runs have no `pulldown` row. Its 10 pulldown runs do (bait or control).
- So `level` never holds a reserved word, and you may refuse one.

**(c) A pool's `individual` is the pool's own label, not `not applicable`** as your 106 example had it.
- PXD058082 is one pool per group, and each pool was injected three times.
- With `not applicable`, its 15 runs would read as 15 pools, not 5.
- `pool_size` > 1 says the individual is a pool. We write `not applicable` in `individual` nowhere today.

**Two conventions for your column descriptions, no change needed:**
- **Pool size as a range:** when the source gives only a range (PXD035446: "2-3 mice per sample"), `pool_size` and
  `pool_size_reserved` are both empty, because the exact number is not known. The range is quoted in `evidence`, and our
  generator refuses a range that is not.
- **`level_value` units:**
  - An `age group` is in years (UO:0000036): months / 12 to 4 places, as `sample_ages.age_years` has it. Our generator
    checks that the two agree for every sample in `sample_ages`.
  - The other units so far are week (UO:0000034) and gray (UO:0000134).
  - A range or a bound ("3 to 6 months", "Age < 28") has no number.

## DATAREPO-83 (ask): a killed build leaves its staged catalog; a full build now takes an hour

**What happened.** The batch's 3600 s publish limit killed three of our builds today (17:43, 18:55 and 19:56 UTC).
- Each left a `.catalog.duckdb.<id>.building` of 20-34 GB. 1.3.2 deletes it when a build refuses, but a killed
  process cannot.
- We deleted the three (76 GB, with our user's OK).
- Our publish script now deletes such leftovers before it builds, when no `datarepo ... build` process is running and
  the file has not been written for a minute.
- We asked PXReprise to raise the limit or move to their 6-hour schedule (PXR-A75).

**(a)** Could `build` itself remove a staged file whose builder is gone (for example, a lock file holding the pid)?
Then every caller is covered, not only our script.

**(b)** Low priority: is an incremental build on your plan, meaning the previous catalog plus the new bundles?
- A full build of 116 deposits (33.6 GB) now takes about an hour, on a hard disk shared with the batch's searches.
- Every publish rebuilds all of it for one new bundle.
