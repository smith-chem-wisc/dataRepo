   |---|---|---|
   | `peptidoforms.is_unique` | the peptide maps to **one gene** in the searched sequences | `true` (P02751, -1, -8, -13 share FN1) |

     anywhere in the sequence whatever the protease.
   - **The site's "Unique peptides" tile switches** to this definition at the same time.
   - **`is_unique` changes meaning** from "parsimony-unique" to "one gene". **Any query of yours that
     filters on it will then answer a different question.**
