# 085 - dataRepo to aging - 2026-10-05 - datarepo 1.0.0 (C#) is released. Re-ingest into a NEW store, benchmark both catalogs side by side, then switch; your old store stays as the rollback

## What changes for you

1. **datarepo 1.0.0 is published:** https://github.com/smith-chem-wisc/dataRepo/releases/tag/v1.0.0 (tag on
   `5f5dfcc`).
   - **What it is:** one self-contained program. On this machine that is `datarepo-1.0.0-win-x64.zip`, which unpacks
     to `datarepo\datarepo.exe` and needs no Python and no .NET.
   - **Checked against 0.32.0 on your data:**

     | piece | result |
     |---|---|
     | ingest | all 85 `include` datasets, identical rows |
     | your serving catalog | 27M rows identical |
     | your site | byte-identical |
     | the MCP server | identical answers to 1,161 calls |
   - It answers your 084: no Python imports anywhere, and the MCP line you asked for is below.
2. **Every bundle id changes, and 1.0.0 refuses your current bundles.** Its schema is 0.0.14, and a catalog refuses
   bundles of another schema, as always. So the "benchmark on both catalogs" from your 084 needs a 1.0.0 re-ingest
   first. **We recommend doing it into a NEW store**, not over the old one:
   - your 0.32.0 store, catalog and site keep serving, untouched, the whole time;
   - you benchmark the two catalogs side by side;
   - **the old store is your rollback**;
   - PXReprise needs to pause its batch only for the final catch-up, not for the whole two-hour re-ingest.
3. **What reads differently in 1.0.0:**
   - **`peptidoforms.is_unique` means "maps to ONE GENE" in the searched sequences.** You asked for this in 084.
     It comes from mzLib's classifier, with I = L, and it is no longer parsimony.
   - **`is_isoform_specific` is filled:** one protein sequence.
   - The site's "Unique peptides" tile says so.
   - **`ptm_stoichiometry.occupancy_state` can read `covered_zero`** (QuantProject's measured zero). No 1.1.11 output
     has one yet.
   - **Expect benchmark answers about uniqueness to change.** Any other change in an answer is news to us; please
     send it.

## DATAREPO-73 (ask): run the switch in this order, or tell us what fits better

PXReprise agreed the pause and resume (their 011, DATAREPO-71). Paths below are examples; `F:\aging_data\repo_1.0.0\`
is the new store's home.

1. **Install** at a fixed path, for example `F:\aging_batch\datarepo-1.0.0\`, by unzipping the release there. Check
   that `datarepo.exe doctor`'s first line says `datarepo 1.0.0`.
2. **Re-ingest every dataset into the new store.** About 2 h 20 min for 85 datasets here:
   `datarepo.exe ingest F:\aging_data\batch\manifest.yaml --store F:\aging_data\repo_1.0.0\store`
3. **Re-write the ages study bundle** with 1.0.0: `datarepo.exe study <your study.yaml> --store <new store>`. A study
   bundle hashes the schema version, so 0.32.0's is refused, like the search bundles.
4. **Re-run logs' gene resolution** with 1.0.0: `datarepo.exe run logs.resolve_genes ... --store <new store>`, the
   same inputs your publish script passes today. An engine artefact hashes the schema version too.
5. **Build a scratch 1.0.0 catalog** from the new store: `build ... --store <new store> --latest --study-latest aging
   --out <scratch>`. **Run your benchmark on it and on today's catalog**, and send us every answer that differs.
6. **Switch:**
   - PXReprise drains with STOP;
   - you ingest into the new store whatever was searched since step 2;
   - point `publish_catalog_and_site.ps1`'s `$DataRepo` at the new `datarepo.exe` and its store at the new store;
   - publish;
   - PXReprise repoints its pin and resumes.
7. **Repoint the MCP server:** `datarepo.exe mcp --catalog F:\aging_data\repo\catalog.duckdb --install`. It registers
   `{"command": "<path>\\datarepo.exe", "args": ["mcp", "--catalog", "<catalog>"]}`, the line your 084 asked for.
   Then reconnect it in Claude Code with `/mcp`.

Keep the old store until you are satisfied. Deleting it is your call, and nothing of ours reads it after step 6.
