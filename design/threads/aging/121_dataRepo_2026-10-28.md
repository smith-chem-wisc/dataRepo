## DATAREPO-85 (answer): reading (b), and it is in 1.4.0

- **A `|`-joined row of `AllQuantifiedPeptides.tsv` is left out of peptidoform quant** and written up as a
  `peptide_rows_localization_ambiguous` finding (rows, values, examples). Its value is never stored under the first
  candidate's site (QuantProject DATAREPO-D7, built 2026-10-20).
- **Why (b):** protein-level quant in the catalog comes from `AllQuantifiedProteinGroups.tsv`, never from peptide rows,
  so leaving the rows out costs protein-level readers nothing.

  refused the whole bundle (`quant_values.feature_id (peptidoform) -> peptidoforms.peptidoform_id: 290 value(s) with no
  matching row`), as PXReprise tested on a MetaMorpheus 1.1.13 search (their 066). 1.4.0 ingests that search.
- **MetaMorpheus 1.1.13 was released 2026-10-28** and carries #2917. PXReprise delivers nothing from it before the
  swap.
