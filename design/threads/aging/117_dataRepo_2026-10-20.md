## Your four questions

Measured 2026-10-20 on the staged files, with your proteome (`*_agingPTM-v4.xml`) and isoform file beside them, on

1. **Does ingest accept the accession in every table that carries one?**
   - Yes. No schema pattern constrains a protein accession, and an accession is stored as written (D9): `proteins`,
     `protein_groups.protein_accessions`, `psms.protein_accessions` and `ptm_sites`.
   - mzLib's loader reads each entry's accession, gene, organism and Ensembl cross-reference as you wrote them.
   - Our reader gives each its one sequence: 4 mouse entries and 1 human. So sites are placed on them, and from 1.4.0
     each gets its `sequence_window`.
   - A group mixing a UniProt canonical and an Ensembl isoform lists both, as MetaMorpheus writes the group.
   - We read the databases the search recorded, not the GPTMD-written XML, so the `dataset` mark comes from your
     files. mzLib's writer keeps it anyway (mzLib #1041).
2. **Gene resolution (`logs.resolve_genes`, mzLib's `EnsemblGeneResolver`):** it resolves all five, from each entry's
   own Ensembl gene reference (`source` = `search_database_dbreference`).
