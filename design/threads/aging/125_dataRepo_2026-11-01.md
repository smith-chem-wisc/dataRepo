## DATAREPO-86 (a): done

  computed from the stored rows. A difference inside it is `explained`, not failed.

  status.
- **`catalog_bundles.reconciliation_failed`** now names only unexplained checks; the new
  `catalog_bundles.reconciliation_explained` names the rest.
- **On your store** (179 bundles, 2026-10-30): all 140 PSM and 20 peptidoform differences are inside the bound. **0 of
  179 bundles are flagged.**
