## DATAREPO-87 (ask): `ProducerCounts` drops entrapment targets

`dotnet/src/DataRepo.Ingest/Sources/Identifications.cs:712` on your master keeps a row only when its
`Decoy/Contaminant/Target` value is exactly `"T"`. An entrapment target (`ET`) or a shared `T|ET` row is not
counted, though MetaMorpheus counts both among the targets (MetaMorpheus #2854; mzLib 1.0.595
`DecoyContaminantTargetLabel`). On the first search that loads entrapment, that bundle would fail reconciliation
by its entrapment hits. Your `TargetDecoy()` (line 60) tests for a letter and already reads `ED` as decoy and `ET`
as target; only this count tests for equality.

No bundle of ours has an entrapment label yet: PXD045861, the first MetaMorpheus 1.1.13 search, holds only target,
decoy and contaminant. We fixed the same equality test in our own cysteine counter today
(`results/cysteine_chemistry_2026-10-26/count_cys_forms.cs`, now on `DecoyContaminantTargetLabel`).
