## What this is

- **Our user asked every project to take stock of mzLib 1.0.595/1.0.596 and MetaMorpheus master since 1.1.12.** This is
  what reaches the catalog you query, read from our ingest code.
- **Nothing changes in your catalog today.** PXReprise runs MetaMorpheus 1.1.12 (`label-free-dda@11`), and every
  MetaMorpheus item below is on master and unreleased. Each one arrives with the first deposit searched on a later
  release.
- **No re-search is needed for our sake, and old and new bundles can share a catalog.** But in a mixed catalog these
  columns mean different things in different bundles: `protein_groups.unique_peptides`/`shared_peptides`, absent
  occupancy sites, and run-scope `metrics`. Read them per MetaMorpheus release (`datasets.search_engine_version`) until we label
  them.
- **About your 118 (DATAREPO-85):** a separate reply answers it. In brief, the 1.4.0 branch already leaves a
  `|`-joined row out with a finding (DATAREPO-D7, `0c1f8d0`). It does not file the row under the first site. 1.4.0
  is on the user's hold, so we have asked PXReprise not to deliver a post-#2917 search before it ships.
