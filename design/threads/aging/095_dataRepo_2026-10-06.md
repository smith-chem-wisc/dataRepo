## Your questions, point by point

1. **Release:** switch on 1.3.0, as above.
2. **Order:**
   - all of steps 3-6 need the re-ingest finished;
   - otherwise they are independent of each other;
   - all of them must come before the build (8).

   Your order works.
3. **The store rename.** We checked the code.
   - **Bundles and engine records hold no store path.**
     - `bundle.json` records the producer's source files, which stay where they are on `F:\aging_data\run_*` and
       `F:\aging_data\db`.
     - An engine's `run.json` records its input files (the database, the gene set, go.obo, the map, the tar), never
       the store.
     - Artefacts live inside the store (`<store>/_engine/...`), so they move with it.
   - **The catalog records absolute paths, as provenance only.** These are `catalog_bundles.path` and
     `catalog_engine_artefacts.path`.
     - Its tables are copies, not views over the store, so a catalog keeps working when the store moves. Only those
       two columns go stale.
     - **The catalog id does not hash paths.** The build after the rename therefore has the **same catalog id** as
       your benchmarked scratch build, with correct paths. Build once more after the rename (9.4); it takes minutes.
     - The 0.32.0 catalog keeps serving after the rename, because its tables are copies. Its provenance paths then
       point at the renamed folder until 9.4 replaces it.
   - **The orthology snapshot is copied into its artefact** (`<store>/_engine/logs.register_orthology/<id>/snapshot/`).
     - The tar in `E:\CodeReview\logs` is read only at registration, and may move afterwards.
     - A query reads the snapshot at `catalog_engine_artefacts.path` + `/snapshot`, so that path must be the current
       store. That is one more reason for 9.4.
   - The manifest's `store:` and PXReprise's copy need no change, as you planned.
4. **Site and MCP.**
   - `site` takes `--out`, `--title`, `--about`, `--base-url`, `--purpose`, `--keyword`, `--notice` as on 0.32.0,
     plus `--data-url` (where the store is served; it turns on downloads and `croissant.json`).
     `D publish <manifest> --site <dir> ...` does the build and the site in one step.
   - MCP: `D mcp --catalog F:\aging_data\repo\catalog.duckdb --install --force`.
     - `--force` repoints an existing entry of the same name (the venv-0.32.0 line) instead of refusing.
     - `D mcp --list` shows what is registered.
     - `D mcp --catalog ... --check` opens the catalog and reports, without serving.
5. **`excluded_runs` and `candidates`:** before the benchmark build (step 7), so you benchmark once.
6. **Benchmark:** as you plan. Nothing more is needed from us before the switch.
