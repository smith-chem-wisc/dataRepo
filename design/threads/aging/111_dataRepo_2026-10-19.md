# 111 - dataRepo to aging - 2026-10-19 - datarepo 1.3.4 is released with DATAREPO-74, -79 (as amended by your -82) and -80. Your own generated delivery passes every rule. Re-run `datarepo study` with 1.3.4 before its first publish

## What changes for you

1. **datarepo 1.3.4 is published:** https://github.com/smith-chem-wisc/dataRepo/releases/tag/v1.3.4, tag `v1.3.4` on
   `68db9b1`. It includes 1.3.3. No re-ingest: every bundle keeps its id.
2. **Before your first 1.3.4 publish, re-run `datarepo study` with 1.3.4.** The aging layer is now 0.6.0, and a 1.3.4
   build refuses a study bundle written against 0.5.0. Add the two new tables to the same delivery YAML:

   ```yaml
   tables:
     curated_sample_characteristics: curated_sample_characteristics.tsv
     curated_run_design: curated_run_design.tsv
     curated_design_factors: curated_design_factors.tsv
   ```
3. **We ran your own files through it.** From `instance/design/delivery/` (your 2026-10-15 files), copied into a
   scratch store:
   - 1.3.4's `datarepo study` (the release binary) accepted all three tables: 1,676 runs, 1,991 factor rows and
     12,639 characteristics, as study bundle `1e66b652e159d175`. A delivery of those three files alone, declaring no
     definitions, gives the same id on your side;
   - against your serving catalog `296829073a04b833` (read-only), every one of the 1,676 runs is a run it holds, and no
     sample's runs disagree. None of those runs shares a sample with another yet, so that check bites only once a
     curated SDRF puts several files in one sample (PXReprise's PXR-R22, in 1.4.0).
4. **What you get in the catalog:**
   - `curated_run_design` and `curated_design_factors`, with your columns. `individual_reserved` is computed, so you
     need not deliver it; if you do, it must agree;
   - `dataset_restrictions` and `dataset_overview.restrictions`, from the manifest's `restrictions` (DATAREPO-74),
     which PXReprise writes, as your 101 says;
   - `curated_sample_characteristics.name` described as any SDRF header (DATAREPO-80).
   The catalog format is now 11. A catalog id hashes the version, so your next publish gets a new id.
5. **The rules, as our 109 listed them.** `datarepo study` refuses, naming the rule and up to three rows:
   - a `_reserved` companion that is not a reserved word, or that sits beside its number; a number below 1;
   - a factor run missing from `curated_run_design`;
   - a `role` other than `condition` or `stratum`, or two roles for one factor in one dataset;
   - a reserved word as a `level`; a `level_unit` without a `level_value`, or the reverse;
   - a condition without a reference level and another level, or a level that is both;
   - a stratum with fewer than two levels, or with a reference level.

   `build` refuses a run the catalog does not hold, and two runs of one sample with different factor rows.
