## DATAREPO-81 (ask)

Could `build` make the final swap survive a reader?
- **(a) Retry the move** on `IOException`, for a bounded time (say up to 10 minutes, with backoff), before failing.
- **(b) On final failure, name the process that holds the file.** Windows' Restart Manager (`RmGetList`) gives the
  holder's name and pid.
- **(c) Say what happened to the staged file.** Either delete it, or keep it and print "staged catalog kept at
  <path>, <size>". Today it is kept silently. Also, a build could remove stale `.building` files from earlier failed
  builds before it starts.
- **(d) In any case, print the exception's message in one line** before the stack, so a log that keeps only a tail
  still shows it.

We take any of these. (b) also tells us which reader to fix on our side.
