## The idea, in plain words

- Each dataset gets its own result for a protein: an effect (log2 change, with a standard error), fitted inside that
  dataset.
- Those results are combined across datasets, label-free and TMT together.
- TMT read at MS2 shrinks fold changes, so the same true change looks smaller in a TMT dataset. The model estimates
  that shrinkage (the **method term**) instead of hiding it.
- What gets stored:
  - the pooled estimate;
  - the label-free-only and TMT-only estimates beside it;
  - the method term the pooled estimate used.

## Scope

- **In now:** protein abundance effects, and QuantProject's hypothesis pooling (`DEF-DIFF-SET-POOL`).
- **Not yet:** PTM occupancy and peptide-level effects from TMT data. They stay label-free only until per-channel
  occupancy is defined.
- **Never:**
  - pooled raw values;
  - DIA with DDA;
  - metabolic labelling (SILAC, heavy water);
  - within-run shares (a compartment's share of a run's intensity).
- **Still in force:** one organism, tissue, stratum and MetaMorpheus version family per pool (our D143). Every TMT
  deposit so far is a 1.1.13 search.

## Guidelines (what the stored data must let a reader do and not do)

1. **A reader can tell a dataset's value scale without guessing.** Every dataset says `label_free`, `isobaric_ms2` or
   `isobaric_ms3`.
2. **A reader can find a TMT sample.** Today one is per channel per run. PXD043296 shows 216 assays with 216
   `sample_id`s: 12 fractions x 18 channels, where it holds at most 18 samples.
3. **A pooled row says what it pooled:**
   - its `method_scope` (`all`, `label_free` or `isobaric`);
   - how many datasets of each method it holds;
   - which method term it used.
4. **A TMT dataset without a channel design is visibly out of the pool** (a status row), not silently absent.

## Usage (what the catalog should tell its readers, on the site, in the MCP descriptions and in findings)

- Quote a `method_scope = all` row as "the effect across datasets", with its count of label-free and TMT datasets.
- Do not set a TMT dataset's per-dataset effect beside a label-free one as if they were on one scale. Compare them
  through the method term or the two per-method rows.
- When the label-free and TMT rows disagree in sign, report both, not the pooled row.
- Never pool `quant_values` across `quant_scale`. That is your 125 rule, kept.
