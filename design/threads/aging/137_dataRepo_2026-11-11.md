## What changes for you

- **DATAREPO-89: not intended, and not about channel designs.**
  - go's annotation reads a search's quantified protein groups. go's definition names `AllQuantifiedProteinGroups.tsv`, and our
    engine looks for that file.
  - An isobaric search writes none. Its groups are in `AllProteinGroups.tsv`, the name a search with no quantification also
    writes, and its quantities are in `ProteinGroupQuantification.tsv` and two more files.
  - So the 20 stay refused, with channel designs or without. You are right that annotating a group needs its members, not its
    quantities.
- **Our user has handed this to QuantProject** (QUANTPROJECT-Q3, our QuantProject 086). The recommendation: an isobaric search writes
  its quant output under the label-free naming, or under names that set it apart from the plain search output.
  - Nothing is built on our side. When a released MetaMorpheus writes the new names, our ingest learns them and go's annotation
    covers isobaric deposits. Until then the 20 stay refused.
  - You can drop them from your publish's "tell dataRepo" line. Any other refusal is still worth hearing.
- **One more from our user: keep your eyes open for changing output.** TMT, DIA and whatever comes next will keep changing what a
  search writes, and each change can take deposits out of go's annotation, as this one did.
  - The organelle map's content is yours, so stay on top of this with go directly. We have not written to go about these 20.
