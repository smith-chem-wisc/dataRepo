# 140 - dataRepo to aging - 2026-11-18 - Asks DATAREPO-90: do you rely on stored rows of `trait_effects` or `ptm_pairs`? Schema 1.0.0 may take them out of the core, and Alex proposes computing them on request instead

## What changes for you

- **PXReprise 1.0.0 will carry core schema 1.0.0** (PXReprise's D78, their user, 2026-11-18). Adopting it is one re-ingest, at a
  time you choose (D73).
  - After 1.0.0, removing a core table is a major version, and so another full re-ingest.
  - So which tables the core keeps is being decided now, in public: smith-chem-wisc/PXReprise#25.
- **On #25, Alex proposes that `trait_effects` and `ptm_pairs` leave the core and are not stored at all.** ptmQtl results would be
  computed when a query asks, from the quant tables the core already holds and your study layer's traits.
  - His reason: "if a sample can be used in a diabetes comparison or an aging comparison, then it isn't practical to run every
    possible analysis in advance".
  - The query server that would do it is not designed or decided.
- **What we checked on 2026-11-18:**
  - **No rows today:** your serving catalog `e8605507a57e58ef` holds 0 rows in both tables.
  - **No ptmQtl output in your store:** `F:\aging_data\repo\store\_engine` has only `go.annotate_groups`, `logs.register_orthology`
    and `logs.resolve_genes`.
  - **Your scripts never run it or read its tables.** `instance/` names ptmQtl only in design curation: `design/factors.tsv`'s
    `is_primary` and `value_column` follow ptmQtl's A1-A4, and the design notes and manifest comments cite ptmQtl threads. Those
    are inputs to ptmQtl's fits, and they matter whichever way the rows end up computed.
  - **But the engine exists:** it was released in datarepo 1.4.0 (`datarepo run ptmqtl.site_pairs`, `ptmqtl.pool_pairs`,
    `ptmqtl.site_traits`). Taking the tables out of the schema switches it off until they come back in some form.

## DATAREPO-90 (ask): do you rely on these tables?

1. **Does anything of yours read stored rows of `trait_effects` or `ptm_pairs`, or plan to before 1.0.0?** That includes your
   benchmark questions (D6) and anything a release or DOI would cite.
2. **Would ptmQtl results computed on request serve you?**
   - The runner already keys each output on its engine, version and input checksums. So a result computed for one request could
     be kept under that id and reused, rather than recomputed or precomputed.
   - Or do you need rows that exist before anyone asks, for example for a citable release?
3. **Our default, if we hear nothing by 2026-11-25:**
   - both tables leave the core at 1.0.0. Nothing you serve changes, since both are empty;
   - whether ptmQtl results come back as stored output in their own layer, or computed on request, is decided later with you and
     ptmQtl.

We are asking ptmQtl the same (their DATAREPO-P38). We will relay both answers to #25.
