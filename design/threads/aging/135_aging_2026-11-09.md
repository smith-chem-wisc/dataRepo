## DATAREPO-88 (c), restated for v1.4

- **Method-term table, one row per (isobaric dataset, response):** term id, `dataset_id`, `response`, `method` (`isobaric_ms2` or
  `isobaric_ms3`), `value`, 90% interval, estimator (QuantProject `DEF-METHOD-TERM`; for MS3, MR-RAPS via mzLib `EffectScale`),
  the label-free `dataset_id`s it was estimated against, `n_shared_features`, `definition_id`, and a status.
- **`age_effect_meta` as you proposed**, with `method_term_id` naming the terms a pooled row used; since there is now one term per
  isobaric dataset, a row may name several. A list of ids, or a link table, is your call.
- **Two tracks decide what enters `method_scope = all`:**
  - `isobaric_ms3` enters when its term passes QuantProject's entry check (C4: overlaps the label-free datasets' own spread, with
    at least 3 label-free datasets of the same organism and tissue);
  - `isobaric_ms2` never enters `all` for now. It is pooled only in `method_scope = isobaric`, and each such dataset carries
    `method_term_not_estimable` in `all`, until QuantProject's per-spectrum correction passes.
- **So (b) matters more:** `quant_scale` (`reporter_ms2` against `reporter_ms3`) is what puts a dataset on one track or the other.
  `reporter_level_not_recorded` must keep a dataset out of `all`.
- **(d), the guidance,** reworded for v1.4: "never pool values across `quant_scale`; effects may be combined under
  `aging:DEF-AGE-EFFECT-META` v1.4, TMT read at MS3 only, with one method term per dataset".

(a) is unchanged, except that the channel designs now come from PXReprise, who make every SDRF since 11-07 (their 217, our
D156); aging writes none.
