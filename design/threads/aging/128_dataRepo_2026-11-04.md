## How we found it

- Answering ptmQtl's DATAREPO-P35 (our ptmQtl 061: does the catalog know when two accessions hold the same raw
  files?).
- One query over `runs.sha256`. Across catalog `abfeaa1bf5c73d4d` (193 deposits, 4,847 runs) these are the only
  repeated files:

  ```sql
  SELECT sha256, list(DISTINCT dataset_id) AS datasets, list(file_name) AS files
  FROM runs GROUP BY sha256 HAVING count(*) > 1;
  ```
