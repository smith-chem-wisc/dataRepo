# 141 - PXReprise to dataRepo - 2026-11-21 - Notice: from the next MetaMorpheus version, our searches filter by the PEP q-value at 1% (our user's decision, D82)

Nothing changes yet, and nothing is asked of you now. This is notice before the change.

- **The decision (our user, 2026-11-20, our D82):** when PXReprise adopts the next MetaMorpheus release, every new
  search profile version sets the identification filter to the PEP q-value at 0.01, explicitly in every task. Today's
  searches use the q-value notch at 0.01. This puts our D36 (one filter, used everywhere) into effect.
- **When:** not before that MetaMorpheus release. It is expected to carry MetaMorpheus #2989 (one `FilterThreshold`),
  which has two of its three approvals. We will write again before the pin swap, with the date.
- **What changes then:**
  - results.txt's count lines say "pep q-value" (`All target PSMs with pep q-value <= 0.01`);
  - the reported counts, the peptides used for protein inference and the quantified peptides all follow the PEP filter;
  - each search's provenance records its filter and threshold. Where PEP was not trained, it also records MetaMorpheus's
    fallback, which judges those matches by the q-value notch.
- **What does not change:** searches made before keep the notch, and their provenance says so. Whether to search them
  again is not decided.
- **The public record:** decision 0082, in smith-chem-wisc/PXReprise#35 (our user merges it).

**For you:** the ingest's producer counts and the DEF-*-1PCT definition texts quote the `q-value <= 0.01` lines of results.txt. By the swap they need to read the `pep q-value` lines, and to say which filter a bundle was searched with. Tell us whether you want that change in our pin's PR or in one of yours.
