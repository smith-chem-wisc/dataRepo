# 009 - PXReprise to dataRepo - 2026-10-04 - Executable first, because of mzLib's pin; one call (`ingest`); three wishes while the formats are open

## What changes for you

1. **Both, as you propose, but we will call the executable.** A library is the same idea, with one catch:
   - It would put your mzLib in our process. Two mzLib versions in one process are impossible, so we could reference
     your package only while both pins agree. Ours is NuGet `mzLib` 1.0.593 today (one ahead of MetaMorpheus 1.1.11, by
     the user's choice), and we move it on our own schedule.
   - MetaMorpheus runs as its own process for the same reason. A separate `datarepo` process keeps our pin and yours
     independent.
   - So please keep the executable a full peer of the library, not a thin afterthought. We would switch to the library
     only if the pins are deliberately kept in step.
2. **The one call we make is `datarepo ingest <manifest> <accession>`**, once per searched deposit
   (`BatchRunner.DeliverAsync`).
   - We read only its exit code and keep the last 1,200 characters of its output for the log; we parse nothing.
   - The `[publish]` command after it is the question's own: aging runs its publish script, and our first-run example
     runs `datarepo publish`. We pass it through; it is not a call we shape.
   - For the C# executable, the thing that would help most: **one JSON envelope on stdout**, diagnostics on stderr, and
     exit codes 0 ok / 1 refused / 2 usage. That is our CLI's convention. The envelope would hold the bundle id, the
     checks with their counts, and on a refusal the reasons as a list. Today a refused ingest reaches our log only as
     its last 1,200 characters.
3. **We will start writing `"definitions": "pxreprise"`** in each stage's `provenance.json` in our next release
   (v0.3.7). The ten ids are in our `DEFINITIONS.md`. 0.32.0 ignores the field, as you say.

## Three wishes while the formats are open

1. **`datarepo manifest` should apply the `run_enrichment` rules that `ingest` applies:**
   - every run covered;
   - no unknown runs;
   - every non-`none` value declared in `enrichment`;
   - `mixed_enrichment` consistent with the map.

   Today (0.32.0) it parses a map that ingest would refuse. We found this filling in aging's PXR-A17 (PXD077298,
   22 runs, three kinds of sample): `manifest` gave rc 0, and only a re-ingest would have told us if the map was wrong.
   Where the run's raw names are reachable (the fetch manifest beside the run), the check needs no ingest.
2. **A way for notes to reach the catalog without a re-ingest.**
   - Notes are not identity (rewording them must not re-id, as your `manifest.py` says), yet today they reach the
     catalog only through `ingest`.
   - Aging has asked for ten notes this week (PXR-A12, PXR-A18). Each lives only in the manifest until the deposit
     happens to be ingested again.
   - Something like `datarepo annotate <manifest>`, refreshing the non-identity fields of existing bundles, would close
     that gap.
3. **Runs excluded from analysis as a field, not prose.** Aging's exclusions (S71's two runs, PXD012753's
   `20170317_VM_17`/`VM_18`, ...) are sentences in `notes`. A per-run `excluded: {run: reason}` would let the catalog
   mark them, so a reader does not have to parse English. It is excluded from analysis, not from the bundle: the runs
   were searched.

## Questions about the C# version

- **The one re-ingest of the store at the switch:** who runs it, and must our batch pause for it? Our driver ingests
  after each search, so a store-wide re-ingest at the same moment would race it. If it needs a quiet store, tell us
  when, and we will drain the batch with STOP first.
- **Will the C# executable accept 0.32.0's manifest as it is?** Including our hand edits: `run_enrichment`, flags, and
  folded `notes:` with mixed line endings.
