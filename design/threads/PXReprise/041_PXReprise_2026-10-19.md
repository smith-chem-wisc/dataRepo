## PXR-R22: read the SDRF the search's design came from

- **Today** the ingest takes the first `*/metadata/*.sdrf.tsv` under the deposit (`Ingester.cs:289-291` at 5804efe).
  That is only ever the deposit's own SDRF.
- **The SDRF the search actually used** is named in the search provenance:

  ```json
  "experimental_design": { "source": "question", "sdrf": "F:\\aging_data\\run_2026-09-21\\PXD047292\\...",
                           "sdrf_sha256": "...", "conditions": [...], "files": 36 }
  ```

  `source` is one of `question` (the question's curated SDRF), `deposit` (the deposit's own) or `pxreprise_default`.
- **So aging's curated SDRFs never reach `sample_characteristics`.** Ten current searches used one, and seven of them
  carry `characteristics[age]`: PXD047289, PXD047292, PXD047293, PXD051203, PXD051644, PXD056458, PXD058248. None of
  those seven has a deposited SDRF, so each bundle got synthetic, unannotated samples (`no_sdrf`).
- **The ask:** when `experimental_design.source` is `question` or `deposit`, read that file (check its sha256), not
  `metadata/`. When it is `pxreprise_default`, keep reading `metadata/` as now: our default carries no annotation.
  - The SDRF's bytes are in the bundle id, so those bundles get new ids; that is correct, since their samples change.
  - Factor values are the question's grouping, while characteristics are facts of the sample. If you would rather keep
    only characteristics from a question's SDRF, say so; we only need the ages and the individuals through.
  - This is not DATAREPO-79 (aging's `curated_design_factors.tsv` as a study table). That one delivers aging's factors
    to aging's catalog. This one puts the facts of the samples the search used into the bundle every question shares.

## PXR-R23: keep the protein-group values of a sample of several files

- **Today** (1.3.1 rule, `docs/ingest.md` "With an experimental design..."): a design sample made of several files
  belongs to no run, so its protein-group intensity, spectral count and occupancy are not stored
  (`sample_quant_not_stored`).
- **That hits nine of the ten curated designs searched so far**, and every one that carries ages. Their designs pool
