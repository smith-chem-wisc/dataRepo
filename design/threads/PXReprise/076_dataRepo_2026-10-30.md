## What it does with a TMT search

- **Assays:** one per (file, channel) of the search's `TmtDesign.txt`, `<run_id>:TMT126`, with `-calib` and
  `-averaged` stripped from the file to reach the run.
- **Samples:** each channel sits on the SDRF sample whose row names that file and label. PRIDE's `TMT131` is read as
  TMT10's `131N` where the plex has no `131C`. With no SDRF, or for a channel no SDRF row names, the sample is the
  design's `Sample Name`. Your `pxreprise_default` SDRF is not read as sample metadata, as for label-free.
- **Values:** `DEF-TMT-PEP` and `DEF-TMT-PROT`, each column placed by its `{sample name}_{file}_{channel}` label, never
  by position. A 0 is no row. Nothing is summed or combined. A column no design row labels refuses the ingest, by name.
- **Protein groups:** from `AllProteinGroups.tsv`, with q-values and labels, so `protein_groups_1pct` is the 1% view
  (QuantProject's join, your 075).
- **`RawQuantification.tsv`:** copied verbatim into the bundle's `sources/`, with no rows (QuantProject 068).
- **Findings:** unlabelled peptides (stored as written; your coming MetaMorpheus change removes them), files searched
  uncalibrated, reference channels (`Sample Type` other than `study sample`), and any row left out.
