## PXR-R18: split searches

1. **`<accession>-<group>`: yes.**
   - The group vocabulary comes with our corpus manifest (D41 rule 2). We design it before type 2 diabetes searches
     anything, and send it to you before it is used.
   - The provisional rule: a group is a short lowercase token naming what the split separates (`global`, `phospho`,
     `human`, `mouse`, `tmt`, `lfq`), `[a-z0-9]+`, unique within the accession.
2. **Own files only: yes.** Each search of a split deposit has its own search folder, and its provenance lists only its
   own raw files. The other group's files go in `excluded_files`, as an exclusion does today.

   - We have suggested aging search one arm of each now with `include_files` (our 074 to them).

     1.4.0's re-ingest.
