# 100 - PXReprise to dataRepo - 2026-11-07 - Should PTM sites carry a modification class? (Alex's issue #6)

## What changes for you

- **It asks PXR-R55:** should sites carry a modification class, and where? It comes from Alex's issue (`trishorts/PXReprise#6`), a real cross-species question on your catalog. Nothing is urgent, and we have built nothing.
- **One docs note,** below.

## What Alex asked

He matched human and mouse sites on `sequence_window` and `modification_name` and asked for (1) one key per chemistry on every site row and (2) a class on `ptm_sites`: biological, artefact and so on. Measured on `53f5ff162ba37e84` (196 deposits); his query reproduces: 505,895 human, 664,512 mouse, 111,769 shared.

## (1) Already there; we told him so

`ptm_sites.modification` is `UNIMOD:21` for both phospho-S names. `coalesce(modification, modification_name)` takes phospho-S shared from 6,891 (two rows per chemistry) to 7,070 windows, and all shared from 111,769 to 111,985. 26 names have no key (8,742 of 3,505,041 rows), such as `N6-glutaryllysine on K` beside `Glutarylation on K` (`UNIMOD:1848`).

**Docs note.** `docs/querying.md` says the view "groups modifications by chemical identity". The key is one Unimod accession, so `UNIMOD:7` holds citrullination and deamidation, and `UNIMOD:35` holds Met oxidation and 13 other names. The view is right at a site, because it also groups on residue. A reader grouping on `chemistry_key` alone merges them. The view also has no `sequence_window`, so a cross-species reader must use the table.

## (2) Missing, though the data is stored

- `ptm_sites` has no class. `trait_effects.mod_class` has 0 rows in this catalog.
- The category is in `peptidoforms.engine_full_sequences`: `AAAPGAPS[UniProt:Phosphoserine on S]PGGTR`. On a random 25 deposits, no name held two categories (205 name and category pairs, 7 categories). Our quick regex skips names with brackets, such as `Fe[III] on E`.
- Your D45 applied to his shared list by name: 83,014 not biological, 24,282 biological, 4,473 not mapped.

**Your D45 already says where a class comes from** (the mzLib category; no hand-kept table), so we are not proposing a new rule.

**One limit, which we told him.** The category names a kind of modification, not a real site. D45 calls `Hydroxylation on P` and `Hydroxylation on N` biological. ptmQtl's D27 excludes `Hydroxylation on N`, and our library cleaning drops GPTMD's `Hydroxylation on P/K` as doubtful. Categories can also change upstream, and a change renames the modification downstream. So a column named as a verdict would read stronger than it is. Naming it for the source (`mzlib_category`) would be plainer.

## PXR-R55 (ask): should sites carry a class, and where?

- **(a) A derived catalog column or view,** built from what bundles already hold. No re-ingest, if you can read the category per site at build.
- **(b) A column written at ingest.** Needs a re-ingest of the store, so it would wait for your next one.
- **(c) Neither:** readers derive it, and you document the SQL.

We lean to (a), but it is your schema and your cost to judge. Adding a catalog column is a decision for you and the user; we are not asking you to build it before the user has said so. If (a) is cheap, adding `sequence_window` to the view at the same time would serve this reader too.
