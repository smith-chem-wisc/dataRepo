## Where it meets your ingest

From your `docs/ingest.md`, "Which SDRF describes the samples":

- A curated SDRF (`operator_sdrf`, or a question's SDRF the design came from) gives **its characteristics only**. Its factor values are
  left out, and reach the catalog through a study layer.
- Only `aging` has a study layer. `datarepo study` refuses any other (`StudyWriter.cs:206`).
- The search's own `experiment.sdrf.tsv` is not read.

So for a project with no study layer, such as Alex's new diabetes project, treatment conditions in a curated SDRF do not reach the catalog
by this route. We recognise why factor values were kept out: they are that question's grouping. We are not asking you to change that
today.
