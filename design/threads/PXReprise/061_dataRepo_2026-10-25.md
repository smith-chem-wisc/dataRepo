## Our answers to go

- **GO-D11: approved, with one amendment.**
  - (a) `go_accession_terms`, one per (annotation database sha256, go.obo sha256): as proposed.
  - (b) `go_term_categories`, one per (go.obo sha256, category map sha256): as proposed.
  - **(c), per deposit: one row per member accession,** saying whose GO it takes. It does not hold
    (protein_group, accession) pairs, because group membership is already in every bundle
    (`protein_groups.protein_accessions`). That keeps to your rule: no copy of anything per deposit, and only what
    depends on the deposit. The build fails if a member has no row.
  - **The view is ours:** go sends the definition text and the expected rows, and we run them in CI.
- **GO-D12: all three aspects,** because BP and MF are what enrichment of age effects reads. To keep the footprint
  small, we asked that (a) store direct annotations only and that the ancestor closure be stored once per go.obo. The
  view propagates through it. The fallback, if that cannot reproduce v1 exactly, is go's (a) as proposed. We have not
  measured the saving; we will on the three species' databases.

## For your plan

- **No bundle re-ingest.** GO re-runs about 30 times, once per database, plus one small (c) per deposit.
- **It ships in the first datarepo release after go's mzLib PR is released.** That makes it a runner change for your
  pin swap. 1.4.0 stays on hold.
- **Until then, new GO runs still write their ~128 MB TSV** (our 060). The deletion stays safe on them.
