## 1. Differential abundance: what each deposit will have

A new engine verb, `differential`, with a batch stage `05_differential/` after the search. It makes one mzLib 1.0.600
`DifferentialAnalysis.Run` call per search, on the search's stored tables:
- label-free: `AllQuantifiedPeptides.tsv`, plus every row of `AllQuantifiedProteinGroups.tsv`;
- TMT: the quantified PSMs and `TmtDesign.txt`.

The comparisons are every pair of the design's conditions. The verb can also run over a delivered run folder (its
`04_search` is kept), with no new search. It is being built now. It ships only if two benchmarks pass: PXD000279
(label-free) and PXD007683 (TMT, compared with R by QuantProject).

**The files QuantProject wants delivered, under mzLib's own names:**

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `DifferentialResults.tsv` | one row per protein group, comparison, `quant_basis` and model: `log2_effect`, standard error, df, p, `p_adjusted`, `status` (`DEF-DIFF-FILE`) |
| `DifferentialResults.metadata.json` | what produced them: `DEF-DIFF v1`, the `analysis_id`, the settings, the input hashes (`DEF-DIFF-META`) |
| `DifferentialDecisions.tsv` | why a protein or a comparison has no row. Without it an absent row cannot be read |
| `StatisticalReport.md` and the figures it links | the write-up. QuantProject's user: every analysis ships one, so it belongs in the bundle |

Also delivered beside them:
- **`DifferentialAssumptions.md`:** QuantProject's assumptions note, word for word, until mzLib's report carries its own
  Assumptions and References sections (their W1).
- **In the stage's provenance:**
  - the search's MetaMorpheus version and identification filter;
  - the samples left out because their condition is `not available`;
  - each lost-transfer file: a file with match-between-runs on and no transferred value, while its siblings have some,
    with its MBR peak count and smallest PIP q-value.

**Two points that touch your sample table:**
- The comparisons use the design's condition only.
- The individual is read from the SDRF the search wrote (`characteristics[individual]`).

A corrected SDRF can change only which channels are reference channels. It is ours to fix:
- **PXD011741 and PXD013708:** their pooled standards are written as "internal pooled standard" and "pooled
  reference", which mzLib does not read as a reference. We will write `pooled`.
- **PXD013708 is delivered,** so it gets a re-ingest with `operator_sdrf`. MetaMorpheus 1.1.15 normalizes nothing, so
  its search values do not change.
