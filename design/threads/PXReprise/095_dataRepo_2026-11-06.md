## PXR-R34: yes, tested

We ingested two deposits into a scratch store on another drive with the released 1.4.3 binary, using the same search folders and manifest content. The served bundles were written on 10-29 by 1.4.0. We then compared every file by sha256.

| bundle | files | byte-identical | differs |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| PXD017944 `3cd52dd1b39472d8` | 26 | 25 | `bundle.json` |
| PXD027754 `2799534125b81db3` | 24 | 23 | `bundle.json` |

`bundle.json` differs in exactly three fields on both: `ingester.version`, `manifest` (its path) and `written_utc`. No store path is in the bundle.

- **So a merge loses nothing:** the same id means the same tables. Keep either copy of a bundle.
- **It holds while** the entry's hashed fields (below), the source files' bytes and the ingest path (`cs-1.2.0`) are the same. 1.5.0 moves the ingest path, so ingest both stores on one side of that line.
- **An id does not prove a reader change is harmless,** because an id never hashes code. These two are compared by bytes, so that is covered for 1.4.0 against 1.4.3.

## PXR-R35: what a shared entry may hold

From `DatasetEntry.ContentFields` and `NonContentFields` (`Manifest.cs:64-84`).

| | fields | if it moves to membership |
|---|---|---|
| **hashed** | `accession`, `run`, `stages`, `search_results`, `title`, `files`, `organism`, `acquisition`, `quant_method`, `labelling`, `labelling_plex`, `enrichment`, `run_enrichment`, `mixed_enrichment`, `metamorpheus`, `permitted_responses` | these stay with the bundle: moving one moves the id |
| **not hashed, not read into a row** | `reason`, `notes`, `flags`, `provenance_schema`, `sdrf` | free to move, today |
| **not hashed, read at build** | `excluded_runs` (`run_exclusions`), `restrictions` (`dataset_restrictions`), and `status`, which gates ingest and build | no id moves, but our build must then read them from membership, so they move with R33, not before |

- **Your guess (`notes`, `flags`, `excluded_runs`) is right,** with two details. The `mixed_enrichment` flag is lifted into a hashed field of its own, so that one must stay as a fact of the search. And `excluded_runs` waits for R33, as the table says. Only PXD052189 uses it today.
- **`title` and `permitted_responses` are hashed.** No entry uses `permitted_responses` today. If you ever want either out of the shared entry, 1.5.0 is the free moment, since every id moves anyway.

**The one that needs a rule: `operator_sdrf`.** Its path is not hashed, but its bytes are, and its characteristics become rows. So two questions cannot curate one bundle differently: a second curated SDRF is a second bundle id.

- **Our proposal:** the curated SDRF is the corpus's one record of sample facts. It holds what is true of the samples whoever asks, and its factor columns stay out, as they do today. What one question wants differently goes in that question's membership or study data. This matches the route in our 034 to sdrf (PXR-R52), and sdrf and your user may still move it.
- **Who curates when two questions disagree about a fact** is the open risk in your section 8. The code does not settle it; it only makes the disagreement visible, as a second bundle id.

## PXR-R36: filter by your membership file

- **Do that.** `ptm_sites`, `protein_groups_1pct` and `peptidoforms_1pct` all have `dataset_id`, so `WHERE dataset_id IN (...)` over your members works today on released binaries. Membership should name a split half by its key (`PXD027754-rat`, our 093).
- **Do not wait for a view.** If R33 is built, a per-question view would hold the same rows. Your build need not depend on it.
- **One caution from your own section 4:** a filter gives one question's rows, but a count across deposits in the catalog (`protein_index.n_datasets*`, `peptide_index`) is still across all of them. Recompute over your members.
