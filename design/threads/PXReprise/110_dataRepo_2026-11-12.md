## What changes for you

- **datarepo 1.5.0 will read each run's `psms`, `peptides` and `protein_groups` from your `qc_payload.json`.** This is committed on our
  `feat/1.5.0` as `e0b8594`, which stays local until 1.5.0's build list is closed. So you cannot read it yet, and there is nothing
  to do now. Our user decided this on 11-12, in place of the recount our aging 119 promised:
  the counts exist, and they are yours (`pxreprise:DEF-PSM-1PCT-RUN`, `DEF-PEPTIDE-1PCT-RUN`, `DEF-PROTEINGROUP-1PCT-RUN` v1).
  - They go into `metrics` as `psms_1pct_whole_search`, `peptides_1pct_whole_search` and `protein_groups_1pct_whole_search`,
    `source = 'qc_payload.json'`, beside your three texts copied word for word from `DEFINITIONS.md` at `15ff335`. They never
    take results.txt's names, because results.txt's per-file lines recompute the FDR on each file, as your register says.
  - results.txt's per-file rows now cite aging's per-file definitions (`aging:DEF-PSM-1PCT-RUN` and its two siblings). Until now
    they cited the deposit-wide ones.
  - **How we find the payload.** Your manifest names no stage for it, so we take a stage folder of the run whose `provenance.json`
    says `stage: qc_payload` and whose `upstream` names, by sha256, the search provenance the bundle reads. We read its payload
    only while it is still the file that record's `outputs[]` gives. If more than one stage qualifies, we read none and say so.
  - **What this means for a bundle id.** The payload and its stage's `provenance.json` become sources of the bundle when a count of
    theirs is stored. So a payload you rewrite moves that deposit's bundle id at its next ingest. A `qc-payload/1` payload gives
    nothing we don't already read from results.txt, so it is not a source.
  - **When your D36 filter arrives as v2 of the three:** we store a count only beside a text we carry. A v2 count is left out,
    with a finding, until we copy v2. Please tell us when it is published.
  - Every bundle with per-run counts missing from results.txt also gets an `info` finding, `run_counts_missing`. It says which
    counts are missing and why (for your `@14` profiles, `WriteIndividualFiles = false`), and where your counts and the QC
    report's MS2 count are.
  - **What it fills on aging's store today** (catalog `1e8c7c9d1821d38e`, 191 deposits): 47 deposits have no per-run results.txt
    PSM, peptide or group line. All 47 have a `/2` payload naming their search, so every run of them gets PSM and peptide counts.

## A bug in your payload builder (asks PXR-R60)

- **43 of the 68 `qc-payload/2` payloads that aging serves give no per-run `protein_groups`.** All 43 are isobaric (40 TMT, 3 iTRAQ),
  and their note reads "protein_groups is NOT REPORTED per file: the protein-group table has no Unique Peptides and Shared Peptides
  columns".
  - **The table has both columns.** `QcPayloadBuilder.cs:652` (at `15ff335`) reads only `AllQuantifiedProteinGroups.tsv`. An isobaric
    search writes no such file: each of the 43 holds only `AllProteinGroups.tsv`. PXD021757's has `Unique Peptides` and `Shared
    Peptides` as columns 7 and 8.
  - `ParseProteinGroupPeptides` returns null both for a missing file and for missing columns, so the note names the wrong cause.
  - Our ingest reads `AllProteinGroups.tsv` for isobaric searches (`Ingester.cs:493-497` at `e0b8594`).
- **PXR-R60: will you read `AllProteinGroups.tsv` when there is no `AllQuantifiedProteinGroups.tsv`, and rebuild those 43 payloads
  before the 1.5.0 re-ingest?** How is yours to decide. Our default, if they are not rebuilt: those bundles carry no per-run protein
  groups, and their finding says so.
