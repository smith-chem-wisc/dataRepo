## What changes for you

MetaMorpheus 1.1.16 was released today at 12:53 UTC (https://github.com/smith-chem-wisc/MetaMorpheus/releases/tag/1.1.16,
from master `ce01ed5a3`, on mzLib 1.0.601).
- **It is PXReprise 1.0.0's MetaMorpheus.** Every search from the swap on is a 1.1.16 search.
- **Nothing on MOZZLE changes before the swap.**

What reaches a bundle:

1. **Peptide counts and peptide q-values move (#2983).** Every peptide-level step and protein FDR now uses each
   peptide's best-scoring match, the one PEP trains on.
   - **Under the q-value notch** (every 1.0.0 profile's filter, D87): on #2983's two notch searches, PSMs and protein
     groups did not change, and peptides rose: PXD000279 35,879 to 37,796, PXD031782 11,710 to 11,834.
   - **Your side:** datarepo 61bb2f2 reads `results.txt`'s counts with `Identifications.cs`'s 1.1.15 notch clause. The
     trial ingests every 1.1.16 arm today, and that is the test of whether it still reconciles under #2983's rule. A
     mismatch comes to you with the arm and the counts.
2. **The quantified peptide list shrinks to the peptides the peptide table passes (#2983).** In 1.1.15 FlashLFQ's list
   held peptides the table fails at 1%: 514 of 12,442 on our trial's PXD031782 arm. The quantified peptide and protein
   tables change with it.
3. **`AllQuantifiedProteinGroups.tsv` stops inventing values (mzLib 1.0.600, #1607).**
   - **Which cell:** a sample median polish cannot place.
   - **Before:** it got the group's reference level. Now it is blank.
4. **`updateSpectralLibrary_*.msp` writes `iRT=`** for spectra kept from an `.msl` input (mzLib 1.0.601, #1625). Our
   chains are `.msp` until the iRT swap, so no 1.0.0 search writes it.
5. **New, and in no 1.0.0 profile: the DIA library search task (#2984).** Its tables (`AllDiaPrecursors.tsv`,
   `AllDiaPeptides.tsv`, `AllDiaProteinGroups.tsv`) come with `dia-1`, after 1.0.0.

**The swap is unchanged (plan step 9):**

- both datarepo pins move together, to the build from the v1.0.0 tag.
