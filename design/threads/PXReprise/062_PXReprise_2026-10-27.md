## What changes for you

- **We are building the split (our D33) now, on the key you proposed and we agreed on 10-11** (your 036, R8; our
  037, R18): a split deposit's searches are keyed `<accession>-<group>`.
- **Six deposits wait on it.** Four already have one half in your store under the bare accession:

| deposit | served now (bare accession) | waiting | split by |
|---|---|---|---|
| PXD027754 | rat muscle, 20 runs (`091f2e19ad452927`) | 18 mouse myotube runs | species |
| PXD013398 | nothing | 10 mouse mitochondria runs and 10 human BioID runs | species |
| PXD057407 | global arm (`38da6b0b18a7a2bf`) | 5 phospho runs | enrichment |

| PXD002467 | 96 protein runs (`2f7bed1517d4ccda`) | 94 phospho runs | enrichment |
| PXD052189 | nothing | 12 label-free runs now, 20 TMT fractions later | label |

- **The rules we propose** (the plan, section 2, has the reasons):
  1. A raw file is in exactly one group of its deposit, so a split's runs never overlap.
  2. A deposit searched once keeps the bare accession. That is every stored bundle but the four above.
  3. **Once a deposit is split, every half carries a group key, and the bare bundle is no longer served.** It stays
     in the store. Otherwise `PXD027754` would mean "the whole deposit" for 170 deposits and "the rat half" for one,
     and a roll-up by accession would be silently wrong.
  4. Each half's provenance lists only its own files. The others go in `excluded_files` with the reason
     `split:<group>`.
  5. Searching the same files twice (PXD073999's side search, your R20) is not a split. It stays out of the store.
- **Your 1.4.0 is on hold with no date** (your 060). Your 036 put the key widening in 1.4.0 so that it would ride the
  re-ingest. We think it does not need the re-ingest. That is R30.

## PXR-R31 (ask): re-keying a served half, and retiring the bare bundle

- **The case:** PXD027754's rat half is served as `PXD027754`. When its mouse half is searched, the rat half becomes
  `PXD027754-rat`.
  - The search is the same, so we want to re-ingest the existing search folder under the new key. It would take
    minutes, against hours for a re-search.
  - Then `PXD027754` must stop being served.
- **The ask:**
  1. Can `ingest` take the `dataset_id` from the manifest entry, rather than from the folder or the accession? A
     re-ingest of the same folder under `PXD027754-rat` then writes a new bundle whose ids all carry the new key.
  2. How should the manifest retire the bare entry so that `--latest` stops serving it? Two forms we see:
     - (a) the bare entry stays, with `superseded_by: [PXD027754-rat, PXD027754-mouse]`, and a build skips it;
     - (b) the bare entry is removed, and its bundle simply has no manifest entry.

     We lean to (a): the history stays readable in the manifest, and `--overwrite` could still bring the bundle back.
  3. Does anything else hold the bare id and need care: the site's existing `datasets/PXD027754.html`, aging's
     curated designs, or a release that already names the bare bundle?

## PXR-R32 (ask): the catalog's roll-up by accession

- **Your 036 named four things that assume one search per accession:**
  - the reconciliation against one `results.txt`;
  - one `dataset_overview` row;
  - the per-dataset indexes;
  - the site's `datasets/<id>.html`.
- **What we would like:**
  - `datasets` (or `dataset_overview`) gains `accession` and `split_group` columns, derived at build from the
    `dataset_id`.
  - One deposit-level view, one row per accession, that lists its halves.
  - The site shows a deposit page that links its halves, and the PRIDE link is built from `accession`.
  - The figures of merit that count deposits count accessions, so a split deposit is not counted twice.
- **The ask:**
  1. Is that the right split between catalog and site?
  2. Is there anything else in your code that keys on the accession? For example the GO and logs artefacts, and
     `ptm_sites`.
  3. Which manifest fields do you want from us: `accession`, `split_group`, `split_reason`
     (`species` | `enrichment` | `label` | `chemistry`) and `split_siblings`? The names are yours to choose.
