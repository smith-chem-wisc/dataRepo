## PXR-R54 (ask): what should a channel marked `empty` become?

**Why it exists.** With no SDRF, our default design called every channel of the kit a study sample, so unused channels were delivered as samples. Three of the four delivered TMT deposits carry them:

| deposit | channels with no sample | what they hold | files |
|---|---|---|---:|
| PXD051031 | 131C; also delivered as `labelling_plex: 11`, and it is TMT10 | 130C's isotope, 0.045 of it | 24 |
| PXD027548 | 130C | 129C's isotope, 0.047 of it | 36 |
| PXD043296 | 132C, 133N (isotopes, 0.03 of their parents), 133C, 134N, 134C, 135N (nearly nothing) | | 12 |

**What we changed (`2df3761`).** From v0.4.41 a default design marks such a channel `Sample Type` `empty`, with no sample name and no condition. The row has to stay, because MetaMorpheus writes a column for every channel of the kit, and you refuse a column no row labels. We found that by trying it: our first version left the row out and 1.4.3 refused the deposit, correctly. An SDRF is left as it is.

**What 1.4.3 does with it,** on real files in scratch stores (`F:\pxreprise_trial_mm-1.1.14\emptychannel2`, ingest rc 0 on all three, 1.1.13 and 1.1.14):

- the finding `isobaric_reference_channels` says "6 channel(s) ... are not study samples: `Sample Type` empty". Good.
- `samples` still has 18 rows and `assays` 18, and `quant_values` holds their values. On PXD043296 Frac1, TMT132C has 5,350 values at a median of about 6,000, where a real channel has about 185,000.

**So the question.** A reader that counts samples, or takes a median across channels, still counts these unless it reads the finding. Should an `empty` channel make no sample, no assay and no values? We would say so, since it names no sample, but the store is yours, and QuantProject may want the values kept for their impurity work. We have told them too.

**The delivered three** say `study sample` on these channels, so they stay as they are unless corrected. We are asking aging. A correction need not search again: the identifications do not change, since TMT10 and TMT11 are one reagent. It could mark the channels in the delivered design and re-ingest, with PXD051031's plex corrected to 10. We have not tried that; it depends on your answer here.
