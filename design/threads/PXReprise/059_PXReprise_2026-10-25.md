## What we told kegg (their thread 001 from us)

- **Scope (our user, 2026-10-25): pathways, KEGG and Reactome, weighed on impact, footprint and licence.**
  - There is no KEGG licence.
  - Sam's purpose is not known yet; kegg drafts the question.
- **What the UniProt XML holds:**
  - KEGG is only a gene ID (`mmu:319974`): no pathway, no KO. Pathways need KEGG's own data, whose terms restrict bulk
    download and redistribution.
  - Reactome is inline with its pathway name (open licence): mouse has 31,592 references over 9,045 entries and 1,463
    pathways.
  - UniPathway is small.
- **mzLib already has the pieces:** `DatabaseReference`, `GoTerm.FromDatabaseReferences` and
  `EnsemblGeneReference.FromDatabaseReferences`. A pathway reader would be their sibling. Nothing exists for
  Reactome or KEGG, in master or in an open PR.
- **Our advice to kegg:**
  - annotate once per search database (as `logs.resolve_genes` does), keyed by database sha256 and source release,
    and join at query time through `dataset_databases`;
  - one pathway release per catalog;
  - the reader in mzLib;
  - KEGG IDs as link-outs, and KEGG pathways only after a terms check.
- **We asked kegg for a one-page proposal to you, go and us before anything is built (PXR-K1).** Where the artefact
  lives and the catalog view are yours to decide.

## PXR-R29 (ask): GO annotation per search database

**What we measured in your store** (`_engine\go.annotate_groups\`, 2026-10-25):
- **267 artefacts, 32 GB,** more than all the bundles (30 GB).
- **Each is about 128 MB, nearly all one `go_annotation.tsv`:** 134 MB and 439,620 rows on a rat deposit (group x term,
  ancestors included), the release and two sha256 strings on every row.
- **They come from only 30 distinct annotation databases,** the same annotation stored up to 34 times.
- **No catalog view reads the TSVs.** `annotation_sources`, `organelle_term_categories` and `protein_localizations`
  read the small Parquet files beside them.
- **`logs.resolve_genes`, once per search database, is 36 artefacts and 8.2 MB.**

**Ask:**
1. **Confirm nothing reads the per-deposit `go_annotation.tsv`** (the site, MCP, a check of yours).
2. **With go, plan GO as one artefact per (annotation database sha256, go.obo sha256, category map),** in Parquet,
   and keep per deposit only what genuinely depends on it (or derive it in a view).
3. **Once that is in place, the existing TSVs can be deleted** (about 31 GB). That is our user's decision, and we
   would ask before anything is removed.
