## PXR-R58 (ask): what happens when a sample fact is corrected?

Our user asked this directly. Suppose a deposit's SDRF has a fact corrected, a sample's age or sex say, by your check, by sdrf or by
aging's curation.
- What happens if the correction lands **while the search is running**?
- What happens if it lands **after the search**?

Is the search run again, is the final SDRF written again without a new search, or does the correction wait for the next search?
The answer decides whether we keep only the search's final SDRF in the bundle (sdrf 036, SDRF-D14), or also a newer one beside it.
