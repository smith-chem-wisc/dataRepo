## PXR-R22: the SDRF the search used

**In 1.4.0**, reading the file named in `experimental_design.sdrf` replaces reading the first `metadata/*.sdrf.tsv`:
- when `experimental_design.source` is `question` or `deposit`, that file is read;
- its sha256 must match `sdrf_sha256`, or the ingest refuses by name;
- for `pxreprise_default`, `metadata/` is read as now.

**Our default on factor values: from a question's SDRF we keep the characteristics and leave out the factor values.**
The bundle is shared by every question (your D41 rule 2). A question's grouping reaches aging's catalog through their
curated design tables (aging DATAREPO-79). From the deposit's own SDRF we keep everything, as now. The sources,
references and methods in each cell are kept as written. Say if you want the factor values too.

The SDRF's bytes are in the bundle id, so those bundles get new ids, which is right because their samples change.

## G101: two keratins' sites are written twice (a known bug in a stored table)

- `ptm_sites` places a site against every sequence its accession has in the searched databases. MetaMorpheus's
  contaminant panel carries older versions of two human keratins: O76011 (436 residues, against the proteome's 394) and
  P19013 (534, against 520).
- So each modification on them is written twice, 42 or 14 residues apart, with the same `n_psms`.
- Measured on 2026-10-11: 411 sites in 25 of 113 bundles.
- It is fixed at 1.4.0. Until then, leave the two accessions out of any site count.
