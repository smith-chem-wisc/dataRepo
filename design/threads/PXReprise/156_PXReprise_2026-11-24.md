# 156 - PXReprise to dataRepo - 2026-11-24 - Notice: label-free-dda@16, label-free-dda-lowres@10 and tmt-dda@7 ship in 1.0.0 (agingPTM-v10 on UniProt 2026_04, cumulative; ion-trap spectral libraries); nothing asked

## What changes for you

**Three new profile versions ship in PXReprise 1.0.0** (smith #59, merged 2026-11-24): `label-free-dda@16`,
`label-free-dda-lowres@10` and `tmt-dda@7`. A deposit searched under them has new databases or a spectral library, so
its identifications differ from the same deposit under `@15`, `lowres@9` or `tmt-dda@6`. Nothing searched before
changes, and nothing is asked.

| version | from | change |
|---|---|---|
| `label-free-dda@16` | `@15` | agingPTM-v10 databases |
| `tmt-dda@7` | `@6` | agingPTM-v10 databases, and ion-trap spectral libraries |
| `label-free-dda-lowres@10` | `lowres@9` | a spectral library of its own (ion trap only); databases stay agingPTM-v3 |

- **agingPTM-v10** (our D91, decision 0091 on smith): UniProt release 2026_04, and the first cumulative PTM build. A site
  found by any delivered deposit with a kept bundle stays, unless a stated quality problem removes it
  (`data/ptm-db/evidence_dropped.tsv`). GPTMD sites: human 7,890, mouse 13,186, rat 2,147 (v9: 6,398, 11,670, 2,049).
  A search's provenance names the database files and their sha256, as before.
- **One spectral library per fragmentation type** (our D92, decision 0092). Ion-trap and Orbitrap spectra never share
  one, and neither do spectra scored as LowCID and as HCD. An ion-trap search reads its own library with neither of
  MetaMorpheus's library switches set, and PXReprise builds the next version from the search's identifications.
  - The provenance's `spectral_library` record gains `fragmentation`, `metamorpheus_mode` (`read`) and
    `built_from_psms`.
  - Measured once, with a library built from a single deposit: PSMs -0.2%, peptides -1.9%, protein groups +5.3%.
    We watch it as the libraries grow (our G132).
- **The q-value notch at 1%, the input SDRF and the MBR threshold** are unchanged from `@15`, `lowres@9` and
  `tmt-dda@6`.
