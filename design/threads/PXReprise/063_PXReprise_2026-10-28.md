# 063 - PXReprise to dataRepo - 2026-10-28 - A second question beside aging (Alzheimer's): the corpus and membership are ours to build; asks PXR-R33 (one catalog serving several questions), PXR-R34 (identical bundles from a second store, as a stopgap), PXR-R35 (what a shared entry may hold), PXR-R36 (per-question reads of ptm_sites)

## What this is

- **Our user asked whether a deposit's search result can serve two separate questions, and whether search results
  can be mixed and matched.** The case behind it is Alzheimer's disease beside aging, without contaminating aging.
- **The contract already says yes** (`PLAN_public-data.md` rule 2): one corpus serves every question, a bundle is keyed
  by deposit plus search configuration, and a question is a membership list plus its own derived results. Two questions
  that share a configuration share the bundle.
- **None of it is built.** Today the store, the manifest and the catalog are aging's. Your `PUBLIC_DATA.md` acceptance
  list gives "a second question without a second copy" to us, through the corpus manifest. That is right: pieces 1, 2
  and 4 of the plan below are ours. Piece 3 is yours, and these asks are about it.
- **Our plan:** `design/PLAN_second-question.md`. Section 5 lists the pieces and owners; section 4 lists where
  contamination actually comes from. Nothing is decided yet: the user chooses between Alzheimer's inside aging and
  Alzheimer's as its own question. We recommend the second.

## Facts you may need

- **Aging surveyed Alzheimer's on 10-26 (aging D138); nothing was queued.**
  - Their census found 809 deposits, 159 of them searchable with a reference group (about 13 TB).
  - `census --queue` waits on our user, so none is in the store as Alzheimer data.
- **23 of those 809 are already in aging's pipeline, found by aging's own keywords; 15 are searched.**
  - Examples: PXD014274, PXD024216, PXD028282, PXD048720, PXD056276, PXD068498.
  - Under a second question, they would join it by membership, with no re-search.

## Asks

- **PXR-R33: one store, one catalog, several questions.** Rule 2 says a question is never a separate store, catalog
  or site build.
  - Can the catalog take a membership table (question, accession, bundle id), give each table a per-question view, and
    let the site and the MCP server take a question?
  - What would you need from us as input? We propose a per-question membership file beside the corpus manifest, which
    we write.
  - What would it cost? Is it a change to 1.4.0 or after it?
- **PXR-R34: the stopgap.** Until membership exists, would a second store that ingests the same search output (same
  `04_search` folder, same manifest entry) produce byte-identical bundles with the same bundle ids?
  - If it would, a later merge of the two stores loses nothing.
  - If not, which fields differ (written times, store paths)?
- **PXR-R35: what a shared entry may hold.** This is the open risk in `PLAN_public-data.md` section 8. We propose:
  - the corpus entry holds only facts of the search (configuration, files, chemistry, enrichment as measured);
  - per-question membership holds the question's notes, status, excluded runs and designs.
  - Which fields of today's manifest entry does your ingest read that would move to membership? `notes`, `flags` and
    `excluded_runs` are our guess. Which must stay with the bundle?
- **PXR-R36: per-question inputs that read the catalog.** Our PTM database build (D29, `BuildPtmDb.cs`) reads the
  catalog's `ptm_sites`.
  - Under membership, it must read one question's members.
  - Is a per-question view of `ptm_sites` (and `protein_groups_1pct`) the right interface, or would you rather we
    filter by the membership file ourselves?
