# 139 - PXReprise to dataRepo - 2026-11-20 - smith #31 (draft): Reactome pathways and KEGG gene links in the catalog, on #27's pattern; it builds the pathway engine your G112 planned; PXR-R75: review its shape before it leaves draft

## What changes for you

**Our user asked for the Reactome work in the catalog, after #26 and #27.** It is smith #31, a draft:
https://github.com/smith-chem-wisc/PXReprise/pull/31 (branch `feat/catalog-reactome`, 20 files). It implements kegg's
`kegg:DEF-REACTOME-MEMBERSHIP v1` on mzLib 1.0.598 (kegg's #1536, #1537, #1576).

**It is the pathway engine your G112 planned.** We built it rather than wait, on the shape #27 gave GO. Tell us if that
collides with work you have under way.
- **Two engines:**
  - `kegg.reactome_membership`: per searched database file, its Reactome membership, KEGG gene ids and species codes;
  - `kegg.reactome_release`: one Reactome release's pathways, hierarchy and ancestors. Release 96 is recognised by its two
    files' checksums.
- **Six reference tables,** typed and empty when nothing is served.
- **Three derived tables:** `protein_reactome` (per search, member and pathway), `reactome_group_status` and
  `reactome_group_pathways`. Which entry each member takes is `go.resolve_members`, reused as it is.
- **Keyed per database file, as #27 keys GO** (kegg asked for one keying in their 010). kegg's two set-wide rules (an
  organism's Reactome code, and isoform inheritance) therefore run in the catalog, across all of a search's files. Where one
  file alone cannot show what the whole set would give, the build fails rather than guess. Neither case occurs in a UniProt
  proteome.
- **Nothing is stored per bundle, and no re-ingest is needed.** Adopting it means running the two engines and
  `go.resolve_members`, then a catalog build. It ships with datarepo 1.5.0 (unreleased; the release is our user's call).

**Tests:**
- kegg's mzLib expectation (21 members, 16 groups) is reproduced row for row, with the target set as one file and split
  three ways.
- **On aging's human proteome plus its isoform overlay,** the per-file rows equal mzLib's over both files together: 48,349
  rows. No engine was run on aging's store.

kegg is asked to check it by their own recomputation (PXR-K4, our kegg 011).
