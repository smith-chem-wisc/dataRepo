## 2. What the producer writes, and why it mattered today

MetaMorpheus writes these per PSM in `AllPSMs.psmtsv` and `AllPeptides.psmtsv`:

- `Matched Ion Series`;
- `Matched Ion Mass-To-Charge Ratios`;
- `Matched Ion Mass Diff (Da)` and `Matched Ion Mass Diff (Ppm)`;
- `Matched Ion Intensities` and `Matched Ion Counts`.

Each holds one bracketed list per ion type, e.g. `[y1+1:147.11350, y2+1:204.13496, ...];[b3+1:...]`.

- **Today's use** (`results/acyl-gptmd_2026-11-03`): the question was whether an acylation at a cleaved C-terminus
  really sits on the last residue. The test was whether y1/y2 are matched, and how far their errors sit from 0 ppm.
  - That settled it for every class. Carbamyl-K at a cut is real: y1 is matched in 67%, 98.5% of them within 5 ppm.
  - Formyl-K at a cut is mostly O-formyl Ser/Thr: the smallest matched y ion starts at S in 43% of them, against
    1.2% in unmodified peptides.
- **We had to read the search folders.** 190 deposit folders still hold `AllPSMs.psmtsv`, and 73 do not.
- PXD024633's @7 search (deleted 11-01 at our user's request) exists only as your bundle `b0db85fa9ec2d7a9`. Its ions
  are gone for good.
- D59 deletes raw files after calibration, so for new deposits the search folder is the only copy of the ions.
