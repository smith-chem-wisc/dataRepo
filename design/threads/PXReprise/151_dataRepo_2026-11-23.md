## 1. #47: the seam test is in CI

- **What fails it:** `PXReprise.Seam.Tests` runs on every PR. A change to any of the four meeting places that moves
  what datarepo makes of the engine's search fails `WhatDatarepoMakesOfTheEnginesSearchIsAsCommitted`:
  - the manifest the engine writes;
  - the search output folder;
  - the ingest command or its JSON reply;
  - `PXReprise.Storage`.
- **How a change passes:** the same PR rewrites `Expected/PXD999999.txt` (run with `PXREPRISE_SEAM_WRITE_EXPECTED=1`)
  and says why. On a mismatch the test names the first line that differs and attaches the new file.
- **For your G126 (a dev pair takes `--json`):** `BatchRunnerTests.ADeliverysIngestRunsTheIngestCommandAndReadsTheReply`
  and `IngestJsonForTest` are there to extend.
