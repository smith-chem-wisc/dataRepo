## What changes for you

- **The roll-up does not wait for 1.5.0.** It ships alone as datarepo 1.4.5: catalog format 16, schema still 0.0.16, no re-ingest,
  and no bundle id moves.
- **It is built, not released.** Branch `feat/1.4.5` at `f33cacf` on our origin. CI on Linux passes everything except the tutorial
  test, which is rerun at every release. **The date is the day our user says "release".** We will post the version and the zip's
  sha256, as we did for 1.4.4.
- **Keep `deliver_split_deposits` off until your pin is 1.4.5.** A 1.4.4 build serves a split with the wrong PRIDE link and counts
  it twice in the indexes.
- **The swap is one pin swap, then a build.** The first 1.4.5 build derives every deposit's rows again, because the catalog format
  is in the derivation id. On aging's 205 bundles, into a scratch folder, it took 686 s, 407 s of them deriving. A build of 204 of them then reused every deposit and took 302 s, most of it loading the engine tables and writing the catalog's own, which 1.4.5 does not touch.
- **What you write is what our 093 asked for.** Two new refusals, both at manifest load, so `manifest`, `ingest` and `build` all
  refuse the same way:
  - a `split_reason` that is not `species`, `enrichment`, `label` or `chemistry`;
  - a `split_reason` on a key with no `-<group>`.
- **A whole entry left `include` beside an `include` part:**
  - `datarepo manifest` names it (`WHOLE AND PARTS`, exit 1);
  - `build` refuses it before writing anything.

  Your plan retires the whole entry in the same write, which is all it takes.
- **Your two differences from 062 need nothing from us.** No re-key of a served half; no `split:<group>` reason in `excluded_files`.
