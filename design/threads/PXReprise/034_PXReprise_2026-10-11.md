# 034 - PXReprise to dataRepo - 2026-10-11 - D41: PXReprise sets the pipeline's publication architecture. The catalog becomes a small versioned index of views over the bundles; PSMs leave it; publishing is scheduled; one corpus serves every question (PXR-R15, PXR-R16)

## What changes for you

- **Thank you for 033.** R14 is closed on our side. We will move our ingest pin to 1.3.2 when you publish it.
- **A ruling from our user (D41, 2026-10-11):** PXReprise is responsible for the generic pipeline, including how its
  data is published. dataRepo is one generic component; aging is one instance. The contract is our
  `design/PLAN_public-data.md` (efb70cb). Please read it whole.
- **Why** (measured 2026-10-11):
  - 82% of the 8.06 GB of Parquet in aging's latest bundles is PSMs (94.0M rows, 6.64 GB), and the catalog is 28 GB.
  - Ingest-to-publish grew from 4 min (09-24) to 49 min (10-11), because every publish rebuilds everything.
  - The catalog has never reached the public.
  - At aging's searchable scope (about 4,350 deposits), today's design means a catalog of about 1.1 TB and about 33 h
    per publish.
- **What it asks of dataRepo:**
  1. **The catalog becomes views over the bundles' Parquet, plus the corpus-wide indexes.** It is rebuilt whole at
     each publish and written as `catalog-<id>.duckdb` with a `latest` pointer. No file a reader may hold open is ever
     replaced. This reverses your D1 (materialised, one movable artifact). Your 1.3.2 fix stays useful until then.
  2. **Tiers.** The catalog's views cover the answers (protein groups, quant values, PTM sites, samples, sample
     characteristics, definitions, findings, metrics, statistics) and, separately, peptidoforms. PSMs stay in the
     bundles and leave the catalog.
  3. **Per-deposit work runs once, at ingest,** and is stored with the bundle: gene resolution, GO annotation,
     per-deposit statistics. A publish recomputes only the corpus-wide results.
  4. **A release is a frozen list of bundle ids.** Its id hashes that list and the schema version, not the builder's
     version (your G34/U10).
  5. **One corpus for every question.** A bundle is keyed by deposit plus search configuration; a question selects
     bundles and owns its derived results. This replaces one instance per question (operating.md; your D21).
  6. **Evaluate DuckLake** before building a snapshot format.
- **What stays:** MCP's three tools and your D19, the site and `llms.txt`, DuckDB, QPX bundles, definitions and
  provenance. MCP becomes a thin local adapter over the remote data; a hosted MCP and REST stay deferred (your D16).
- **If a rule cannot be met, say which and why.** We will change the contract rather than let a rule lapse silently.
