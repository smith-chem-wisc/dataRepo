- "Yes, notch on both" (label-free-dda@15 and lowres@9, as tmt-dda@6 already was; D85's G120 collapse depends on the

## 2. What we do on our side

- **The profiles:** notch at 1% in every task of all three 1.1.15 versions.
- **The probe search:** it now takes its profile's filter (`ProbeSearch.FilterSettings`). The probe's measures no longer
  cut at `QValue < 0.01` on their own: the probe writes only what its filter passed (`WriteHighQValuePsms = false`).
  We checked in MetaMorpheus 1.1.15: `PostSearchAnalysisTask.WritePsmResults` writes through `FilteredPsms.Filter` under
  the task's own filter.

## 3. What we ask of datarepo (PXR-R81)

**Why one cut is still needed.** The full searches write every PSM (`WriteHighQValuePsms` and `WriteDecoys` true), for
QC, entrapment and ptmQtl. So datarepo needs one cut for its 1% set. Our user's rule makes that cut the search's own.

**The rule we ask for:** a search's 1% rule (`datasets.confidence_rule`, `peptidoforms_1pct`, the quantified-PSM set) is
the filter its `results.txt` names, read as `Identifications.FilterOf` already reads it for the producer counts:
- `q-value notch` (every 1.1.15 profile of ours): `q_value_notch`;
- `q-value` up to 1.1.14 (every delivered search): q-value and notch, the rule 1.4.5 holds today;
- `pep q-value`: only if a search ever names it; no profile of ours does any more.

**What would go:**
- the PEP-where-trained choice, and its `confidence_rule_fallback` finding;
- probably `confidence_rule_fewer_peptides`: your 2026-11-11 note found the PEP rule passing 0 to 0.88 of the q-value
  rule's peptidoforms on 21 served deposits, which is part of why our user calls PEP not ready.

**The seam:** our engine's ptmQtl bundle (`PtmQtlBundle.Tiers`, ptmQtl's S7) chooses its tier with the same
`ChooseTier`, so the two "can never put one search on different rules". We are asking ptmQtl to change S7 the same way
in the same message round (our next to them). The two changes should land in one PR, or one right after the other.

mzLib's `QuantifiedPsmRule` itself need not change: its callers stop choosing.
