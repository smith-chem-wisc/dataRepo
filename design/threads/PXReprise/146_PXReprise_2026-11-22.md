## 2. PXR-R79: the facts, from `feat/differential`

The verb is built (`pxreprise differential --search <04_search> --out <06_differential> [--sdrf <corrected SDRF>]`), with
a batch stage after the search. It ran on two real searches as smoke tests (not the benchmarks):

| search | analysis | result |
|---|---|---|
| PXD031782, label-free, the 1.1.15 trial | 12 samples in 6 conditions, 15 comparisons, both bases | 38,595 rows per basis; 223 (MS/MS only) and 273 (with transfers) at `p_adjusted` <= 0.05 |
| PXD019690-astro, TMT10, delivered | 10 samples, one comparison | 4,482 rows; 9 at `p_adjusted` <= 0.05 |

**(6) The folder is `06_differential`, not `05_*`.** Its `provenance.json` says `stage: differential`, and no search
lists it upstream.

**(2) The input hashes in `DifferentialResults.metadata.json`:** by ROLE, using the bundle's own source names, each the
sha256 of the whole file's bytes (as `BundleWriter.AddSource` hashes):

| | roles |
|---|---|
| label-free | `experimental_design`, `peptide_quant`, `protein_group_quant` |
| TMT | `isobaric_design`, `isobaric_raw_quant` (RawQuantification.tsv), `psms`, `protein_groups` (AllProteinGroups.tsv) |
| no bundle counterpart | `observation_table`, `written_sdrf`, `corrected_sdrf` |

- `observation_table` is always first. It is mzLib's hash of the table it built, not of a file, so skip it when
  binding.
- `written_sdrf` (the search's `experiment.sdrf.tsv`) is listed only when individuals were read from it. The bundle's
  `sdrf` is the operator or curated SDRF, not this file.
- On PXD031782, the label-free hashes equal the trial's scratch bundle's recorded `experimental_design`, `peptide_quant`
  and `protein_group_quant`. The bundle records `experimental_design` only when it placed the sample columns by sample.

**(3) The protein group:**
- `feature_id` is MetaMorpheus's "Protein Accession" cell, verbatim (e.g. `A0A075B5J6|P01629`).
- `feature_accessions` holds the same accessions joined by `;`.
- TMT is the same: its PSMs take their groups from `AllProteinGroups.tsv`'s Unique and Shared Peptides lists.

**(4) Every `status` value:**
- `fitted`, `fitted_single_peptide`;
- `absent_in_numerator`, `absent_in_denominator`, `absent_in_both`;
- `not_converged`;
- `below_support:no_unique_peptides`, `below_support:min_2_per_side` (`min_N_per_side` under other settings),
  `below_support:too_few_observations`;
- `not_estimable:confounded`, `not_estimable:rank_deficient`.

**`p_adjusted` adjusts within one Benjamini-Hochberg family** per analysis, stratum, grain, quantity, `quant_basis`,
comparison and method, over fitted rows (mzLib's `DifferentialWriting` FamilyKey). So it is within one comparison and
one basis. Every row's method is `moderated`, so a family pools the mixed-model, reduced and moderated-t rows. (We
correct QuantProject's "and model" in 155 to them too.)

**(5) The stage's `provenance.json` fields:**
- top level: `schema` (`pxreprise-differential/1`), `stage`, `started_utc`, `host`, `tools{pxreprise,mzlib,dotnet}`,
  `definition`, `method_version`, `quant_style`, `match_between_runs`, `analysed`, `assumptions`, `finished_utc`;
- `search{dir,task_folder,pipeline,metamorpheus,identification_filter}`;
- `design{file,sha256,samples,conditions}`;
- `corrected_sdrf{sdrf,sdrf_sha256,design,design_sha256,references}`, or null;
- `individuals{source,reason | sdrf,column,individuals}`;
- `samples_left_out[{sample,condition,files | channels}]`;
- `lost_transfer_files[{file,mbr_peaks,min_pip_q_value}]`;
- `analyses[{name,quant_basis,dir,analysis_id,samples,rows,by_status,contrasts,decisions,design_warnings}]`;
- `findings[{kind,detail}]`;
- `inputs[{path,size_bytes,sha256}]`.

**Layout:**
- **One runner call per basis.** The runner takes one table, and its metadata describes one analysis. Label-free
  writes `msms_only/` and `mbr_kept/`; TMT writes `isobaric/`.
- **Each folder** holds mzLib's files under mzLib's names, plus `DifferentialAssumptions.md`.
- **The stage's `provenance.json`** sits at the top.
