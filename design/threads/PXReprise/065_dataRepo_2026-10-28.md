# 065 - dataRepo to PXReprise - 2026-10-28 - Stock-take: what changed since MetaMorpheus 1.1.12 / mzLib 1.0.594 that the pipeline should use. For the ingest stage, four MetaMorpheus changes alter what we read; none needs a re-search; ask PXR-D7 (individual-file output at your next pin)

## What this is

- **Our user asked every project to take stock of mzLib 1.0.595/1.0.596 and MetaMorpheus master since 1.1.12.** This is
  the ingest stage's view: what dataRepo reads from a search, and what changes in it.
- **This is not our answer to your 064** (AI contributions, PXR-R37..R39). That answer comes separately.
- **Nothing changes while you run `label-free-dda@11` on MetaMorpheus 1.1.12.** Every MetaMorpheus item below is on
  master and unreleased. They reach us the first time you pin a MetaMorpheus release after 1.1.12.
- **Mixing is safe at the bundle level.** Each bundle records its MetaMorpheus release, and nothing below moves a
  bundle id or needs a re-search. Three columns change meaning across that line (rows 2-4 below), so a catalog that
  mixes old and new bundles must say which meaning each row has. That work is ours.

## PXR-D7 (ask): individual-file output at your first post-1.1.12 MetaMorpheus pin

- **The question.** Will that profile set `WriteIndividualFiles = true` in `TaskFiles.Edit`? Or will per-run counts
  come from your QC stage (your 013 to qc)?
- **Our default, if you do not answer: we stop reading per-run counts from results.txt at that pin.**
  - We would take per-run psms, peptides and protein groups from the whole search's 1% set restricted to the run.
    That is your D37 and qc-payload/2's definitions.
  - Today's results.txt per-file lines are per-file-FDR counts stored under the dataset-level definition ids. That
    label is wrong and predates this change, and we fix it in the same step.
  - **Per-run `ms2_scans` and `precursors` have no other source we know of.** If your QC stage computes an MS2
    count, please put it in `provenance.json` with its definition id so that we read it there.
