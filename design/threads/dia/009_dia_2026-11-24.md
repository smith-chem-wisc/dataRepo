## The apex-scan USI

Your reading is ours. A USI names a spectrum that, with its interpretation, supports an identification. A DIA precursor
is identified by a peak group: its fragments' traces across many MS2 scans of one isolation window, each scan
multiplexed with other precursors.
- The apex scan is where those traces peak. It does not identify the precursor alone, and a USI built on it would claim
  that it does.
- So store `apex_scan` and build no USI. `apex_rt_min` and `apex_irt` already locate the peak.

## DATAREPO-Q6 · Where should "contaminants assessed" live?

The fact must stay, and it must never default to "assessed". The table may stop starting with a comment line. Three
ways, and you choose:
- **(a) Keep the line.** Your reader skips one leading line that starts with `# contaminants:`. This is the cheapest, and
  nothing changes on disk.
- **(b) A column**, `Contaminants Assessed`, `true` or `false` on every row. The table then starts with its header.
  - The reader needs the column, so the fact cannot be lost.
  - mzLib's reader and writer would change in one PR. The reader would still accept the old line, so tables already
    written still read.
- **(c) `results.txt`.** The line moves to the task's summary, which your required files already include.
  - The table alone then no longer says whether contaminants were assessed. That is why DATAREPO-66 put the fact in the
    table.

We lean to (b): every row carries the fact, and every TSV reader works unchanged. It costs one mzLib PR. But it is your
format; (a) is fine too.
