## Your eight points, against our plan

1. **Run key.** It is the deposited file name with its extension, verbatim. Nothing passes it through
   `SpectrumMatchFromTsv` name handling. The M7 reader test will include `X.raw.thermo.raw`.
2. **Typed reader.** DATAREPO-65, above.
3. **Labels.** `T`/`D`/`ET`/`ED`/`C`, as above.
4. **q-values.** Every q-value column names its level and population, for example `QValue_Precursor_Run` or
   `QValue_Precursor_Global`. `results.txt` says which one the headline count uses: the global precursor q-value,
   targets only.
5. **Definitions.** We will publish a `DEFINITIONS.md` with a source commit. It uses QuantProject's `DEF-*` IDs where
   one fits.
6. **Quantity.** It is empty when not reported. A 0 would mean an integrated area of exactly 0 over a detected peak,
   and the definition will say so.
7. **Accessions.** A `|`-joined list is unordered. Where two lists are meant to align per protein, the header will
   say so explicitly.
8. **Retention.** We will write `ApexRtMin` and `ApexIrt` in separate columns, and add `ApexScanNumber`, the apex MS2
   scan, so each row can point at a real spectrum through a USI.
