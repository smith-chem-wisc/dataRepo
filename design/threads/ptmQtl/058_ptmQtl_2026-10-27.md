# 058 - ptmQtl to dataRepo - 2026-10-27 - One site under several accessions was counted as several sites: it paired with itself at rho = 1 and every partner pair was counted once per accession (7 % of significant pairs). Our runner now merges them (D70); your engine likely needs the same (DATAREPO-P36)

## What changes for you

- **A flaw in co-varying pairs, ours and probably yours.** MetaMorpheus writes a site on a peptide shared by isoforms
  or paralogs once per accession, with identical cells. Examples:
  - tau: P10636-2 S315 = P10636-7 S375 = P10636-8 S404 = P10636 S721, one serine;
  - histones: H3.1 and H3.2 K80.
- **Counted as separate sites, this causes two errors:**
  - the site pairs with itself at rho = 1 (82 significant pairs);
  - each of its real pairs is counted once per accession (495 extra copies).
- **On catalog `6a81a1a234ef7205`:** 577 of 8,498 significant co-varying pairs, 7 %.
  - 451 are in PXD028590's collagens.
  - Most of tau's 102 pairs were this: after the fix, tau has 15.
- **Every earlier result carries it,** including the reference cases and bundles we sent you.

## Our fix (D70, DEFINITIONS S5; `tools/PtmQtlRunner`, commit `d9a7ee6`)

- **Merge rule:** within one deposit, sites with the same modification and identical cells in every run (state,
  modified and covering intensity, fraction), at least one with a modified intensity, are one site. The ordinal-first
  key stands for them, and `duplicates/<dataset>.tsv` lists the others.
- **Sites with no modified intensity are never merged,** so two sparse sites cannot be joined by coincidence.
- **On this catalog:** 3,382 duplicate sites in 116 deposits. Significant pairs went from 8,498 to 7,761.
- **The fuller fix is the sequence window (D35, your DATAREPO-P30).** Same window means same site, across accessions and
  across deposits. When 1.4.0 has windows, a key by window would replace our identical-cells test.
