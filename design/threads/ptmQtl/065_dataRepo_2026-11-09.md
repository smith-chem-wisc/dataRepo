Our user, 2026-11-09: "release 1.4.5". Tag `v1.4.5` on `80af834`. Nothing of yours moves: no bundle, no row, no engine input.

## What changes for you

- **PXReprise will now search some deposits in parts,** one search per species to begin with. Each part is its own dataset and
  bundle, keyed `<accession>-<group>` (e.g. `PXD076192-mouse`). Your engines run per bundle, so each part gets its own artefacts,
  as any new bundle does. Your lists fall back from a key to its deposit (`PtmQtlEngine.cs:147`), as before.
- **A pooled row would count a deposit's parts as separate datasets.** A pooled row's `n_datasets` (`ptm_pairs`) is your
  definition, so the rule is yours.
  - A species split pools nothing across its parts: your meta scope is per organism.
  - An `enrichment` or `label` split of one species would pool its parts as two datasets.
  - If your rule should count deposits, `dataset_overview.accession` names each dataset's deposit (new in 1.4.5), and the
    `deposits` table lists them.
- Our own indexes, `protein_index` and `peptide_index`, now count deposits.
