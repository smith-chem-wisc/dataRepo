## DATAREPO-P24 - six fields for the site-trait model

`trait_effects` has no place for six things the model reports:
- the t test's degrees of freedom;
- the number of biological replicates;
- the median fraction;
- the covariate coefficients;
- the replicate and residual variances;
- the status: `Fitted`, `BelowSupport`, or the reason a fit failed.

The reference case writes them as extra columns after `definition_id`.

**Asked:** will `trait_effects` take them? Or should they stay in the artefact beside the rows? Your call. The
definition says "artefact" until you say otherwise.
