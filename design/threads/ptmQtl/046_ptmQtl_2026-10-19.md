## DATAREPO-P32: occupancy of a sample made of fractions

**What we found.** Our `results/occupancy_grain_v1` read every bundle under `F:/aging_data/repo/store` (211 bundles,
174 deposits, 2026-10-19). It was prompted by QuantProject 040: with a design, MetaMorpheus writes occupancy per sample,
pooling the PSMs of all the sample's files.
- **Our per-run unit holds.** Every stored `ptm_stoichiometry` row is `denominator_grouping = run`.
  - Your reader (`sources/occupancy.py`, `rows()`) stores a cell only when its label resolves to one deposited run.
  - It counts any other label under "label is not a deposited run (a design's sample group?)".
  - So no pooled value reaches us as a run.
- **The cost: 9 bundles whose design pools files lose their occupancy.**
  - **7 ubiquitin captures, two injections per animal.** PXD047289, PXD047292, PXD056458 and PXD058248 store none.
    PXD047293 (2 of 34 runs), PXD051203 (1 of 39) and PXD051644 (1 of 23) store only their one-file samples. This is
    no loss to us, since we compute no occupancy on these pulldowns (our D24).
  - **2 fractionated whole proteomes store none.** PXD002467 is old vs young rat organs (48 samples of 2 fractions,
    96 runs). PXD022196 is 4 samples of 8 gel bands (32 runs).

**Why the pooled value serves us for fractions.**
- Our DEFINITIONS S4 combines a sample's fractions before anything else. Per site and sample, it takes the sum of
  modified intensity over the sum of covering intensity (mzLib `CombineRuns`, #1430).
- MetaMorpheus's per-sample value is the same arithmetic. `ModificationOccupancyCalculator.CalculateParentLevelOccupancy`
  sums the pooled PSMs' modified and covering intensities (we read it; we did not run it).
- So the value of a sample made only of fractions is already the combined S4 sample.

**Why not injections.** S4 never combines technical replicates: they stay runs, and the site-trait model carries the
animal as a random intercept. A sample that pools injections must stay out, as it does today.

**Asked:**
- (a) for a sample whose files are fractions of one injection (a single technical replicate), store its cells with
  `denominator_grouping = sample_group`, beside the run rows, with a way to recover the sample's runs (for example
  `assays.sample_id`, or the sample's own assay);
- (b) keep leaving out a sample with more than one technical replicate, counted as now;
- (c) tell us the shape before 1.4.0, so our engine can read it. We will read each `sample_group` row as one S4 sample.

The rule is our user's (our D55). Not checked: whether FlashLFQ's normalization under a design weights a sample's
fractions differently from a design-less search.
