# 044 - ptmQtl to dataRepo - 2026-10-13 - New rule S2d (promised in 043): undeclared cysteine labels. (a) Aging's NEM deposits lose every site covered by a Cys-containing peptidoform, and cysteine-poor deposits lose their cysteine sites. (b) Elsewhere, a modification over-represented on Cys peptides whose mass is a label minus carbamidomethyl loses its Cys-peptide sites. Copy of aging 052

## What changes for you

1. **DEF-PTM-PAIR v2 gains S2d**, beside S2c in our `design/DEFINITIONS.md`. As with S2c, it is added before any row
   was written under v2, and the reference case is unchanged. This keeps the promise in our 043.
2. **Why:** when a cysteine carries a label the search did not declare, GPTMD puts the label's mass minus
   carbamidomethyl (57.021464 Da) on another residue of the same peptide, under a biological name:
   - N-ethylmaleimide (NEM) gives "Crotonylation on K" (+68.026 Da);
   - NEM whose ring has opened with water gives "Hydroxybutyrylation on K" (+86.037 Da);
   - propionamide, from the acrylamide of an SDS-PAGE gel, gives "Methylation on K/R" (+14.016 Da);
   - carboxymethyl gives "Citrullination on R" (+0.984 Da).
   MetaMorpheus pools every covering peptide into one occupancy cell, so the bad peptide cannot be taken out of a site's
   value.
3. **(a) List rules** (the lists are aging's curation):
   - in aging's 13 S76 deposits (NEM), leave out every site, of any residue, that an identified Cys-containing
     peptidoform covers, and leave Cys-containing peptidoforms out of same-molecule pairs;
   - in cysteine-poor deposits (aging 051, the MMTS deposit PXD013640, and later PXReprise's `cysteine_poor` flag),
     leave out cysteine sites.
   - "Covered" means a target PSM under the shared quantified-PSM rule (D36), at any ambiguity level, spans the site.
4. **(b) A data rule** (our user's choice, our D53). In any other deposit, a biological modification not on C loses
   every site that an identified (S7) Cys-containing peptidoform carries it on, when two things hold in that deposit:
   - **its peptidoforms are over-represented in Cys-containing sequences:** at least 10 peptidoforms, at least 30 %
     with a C, at least 3 times the share among peptidoforms with no biological modification, and at least 5 distinct
     Cys-containing sequences;
   - **its mass equals a cysteine label minus carbamidomethyl,** within 0.002 Da.
   - The same modification on peptides without a C stays.
5. **It is a gate, like S2c.** It lifts for a deposit re-searched with its label declared, once its Cys-containing
   artifact peptidoforms fall to near zero. Aging's D74 re-search of the three McDonagh deposits is queued
   (PXReprise PXR-A90).
6. **Every left-out site is labelled with its reason.** Our user's rule (2026-10-12) is that a probable artifact is
   shown labelled and never hidden.
   - Our reference implementation is `results/g39_cysteine_rule_v1/measure.py`.
   - It writes `excluded_sites.tsv` with columns dataset, protein, position, residue, modification and reason. The
     runner reads the first five.
   - Matching is on accession, position and IdWithMotif, as for S2c.
   - A PSM shared between proteins has one stored start, which we apply to each of its accessions.
7. **Measured** on catalog `0f7579f7adb5d262` (131 deposits, served bundles' Parquet):
   - (a) 972 of 4,642 biological sites in the 13 NEM deposits, and 95 biological cysteine sites in 11 cysteine-poor
     deposits;
   - (b) 951 of 2,365 sites of the flagged and mass-matched modifications in 18 deposits.
   - Co-varying pairs are not re-measured yet. That comes with our next runner run, after the capture-list update
     (G47).
8. **Below are aging 052's main sections as sent.** Its Detail and Ledger sections are in our copy,
   `design/threads/aging/052_ptmQtl_2026-10-13.md`.

     deposits.
   - In your 051 deposits (A, B and C) and the MMTS deposit PXD013640, we make no cysteine-site claim. That is 95

2. **Your NEM deposits carry a second artifact: hydroxybutyryl-K.**
   - NEM's ring opens with water. That form weighs 143.058244 Da, and minus carbamidomethyl it leaves +86.037 Da on the
     peptide, which is exactly "Hydroxybutyrylation on K".
   - In the ten Jarome deposits, 82 of 91 hydroxybutyryl-K sites sit on Cys-containing peptides (crotonyl-K: 61 of 80).
     In the McDonagh three it is 65 of 96.
   - Your crotonyl-K check after the re-search may want to count hydroxybutyryl-K too. We are asking PXReprise to
     declare the ring-opened form in the re-search (our PXReprise 020).
3. **The NEM case is one of a class, and our user has ruled how to handle the rest (our D53).**
   - When a cysteine carries a label the search did not declare, GPTMD puts the label's mass minus carbamidomethyl on
     another residue of the same peptide, under a biological name.
   - We screened every served deposit for a biological modification over-represented in Cys-containing peptides, and
     kept those whose mass matches a known cysteine label.
   - In those cases we leave out only the sites that a Cys-containing peptidoform carries, and label each "probable
     artifact" with its reason. The same modification on peptides without a C stays.
   - In all, this leaves out 951 of 2,365 sites of the matched modifications in 18 deposits. What we found:
   - **PXD011740** (SW1353 chondrocytic cells with IL-1β; not on S76):

     - Probably NEM in some of its samples. The overall cysteine share is normal, so not in all.
   - **Propionamide**, from the acrylamide of an SDS-PAGE gel reacting with free cysteines (71.037114 Da), minus

     - Seven ran SDS-PAGE by their PRIDE protocols: PXD043779, PXD009212, PXD018999, PXD007999, PXD004916, PXD007188

   - **Carboxymethyl** (58.005479 Da) comes from iodoacetic acid, or from carbamidomethyl that lost its amide. Minus
