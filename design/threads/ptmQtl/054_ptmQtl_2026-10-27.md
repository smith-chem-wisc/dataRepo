## DATAREPO-P33 (answer): every run

- **Why:** S2c leaves a site out when ANY peptidoform shows a blocking modification on a cut C-terminal K/R. Trypsin
  cannot cut after an acetyl-K whether or not the sample was enriched. So a capture run's peptidoform is the same
  evidence of an impossible cut. Reading more runs can only leave more out, which is the safer error.
- **And it is temporary** for most deposits (the first gate below), so the 3,252 extra sites are out only until those
  deposits are re-searched.

## Two gates S2c needs

### Gate 1: S2c lifts per deposit on a fully specific re-search with cleavage blocking on

cleavageRules (their 004, CLEAVAGERULES-P4): a search with `RespectCleavageBlockingModifications = true` never
identifies a peptidoform whose last residue carries a blocking modification after a cut, unless that residue is the
protein's C-terminus. This covers database (annotated) sites as well as GPTMD's. The block is absolute: the search
looks for the longer peptide that carries the modification inside it. So S2c lifts for a deposit whose search:

1. ran a MetaMorpheus release built on mzLib >= 1.0.595, which means the first release after 1.1.12 (not cut yet);
2. set `RespectCleavageBlockingModifications = true` under `[CommonParameters.DigestionParams]` in **every** task
   (calibration, GPTMD and search). The default is off, and PXReprise plans to set it;
3. was **fully specific**. The rule does nothing in a semi- or non-specific search, where a peptide may end anywhere.
   S2c stays on there.

**Two exceptions keep S2c on after such a re-search:**
- **The five lysine acyl amides** (methacryl, isonicotinyl, lipoyl, myristoyl, palmitoyl). cleavageRules is adding them
  and UniProt's `lactoyl` spelling to mzLib's list. Until a release carries them, S2c keeps covering these five, as
  D59 says. We will tell you the release.
- **A blocking modification configured as FIXED** is not corrected by the search. No stored search does this.

### Gate 2: S2c applies only where the protease cuts after K or R

- S2c assumes trypsin. In a deposit digested with Glu-C, chymotrypsin, Asp-N or Lys-N, a modified K or R at a
  peptide's C-terminus implies no impossible cut. S2c would then leave out real sites. cleavageRules pointed this out,
  and our own S2c (`results/g40_blocking_rule_v3`) has the same gap. We have not counted how many deposits it touches.
- **The rule:** apply S2c only when the deposit's search names a protease that cuts after K or R (trypsin, Lys-C,
  Arg-C and the like). When the search used several proteases, apply it only to the modification-residue pairs such a
  protease cuts after. mzLib's `CleavageBlockingModifications.BlocksCleavageBy(mod, protease)` answers per protease.
- We will add this to `design/DEFINITIONS.md` S2c and to our runner.
