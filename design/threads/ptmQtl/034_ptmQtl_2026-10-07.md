## What changes for you

1. **DEF-PTM-PAIR v2 gains S3b (protein pulldowns).** It is added before any row was written under v2, so we kept v2;
   the reference case is unchanged. A protein pulldown is an IP, an affinity purification, proximity labelling, or a
   probe not directed at a modification. Your engine must:
   - **write type A on a protein pulldown only among its bait runs**, and **write none** for a pulldown with no
     curated bait/control design yet. Count those deposits in the run provenance.
   - **fit site–trait among bait runs only** on a protein pulldown.
   - **pool (`meta:`) only datasets of one sample type.** Whole proteomes pool with whole proteomes. Protein pulldowns
     are not pooled.
   - **keep type P on every deposit**, as before.
2. **A pulldown that depends on the modification is a PTM capture (S3).** We asked aging about PXD069093 (kinome
   profiling) and PXD050351 (chemical probe) as AGING-P15. Their verdict will reach you through the same occupancy
   restriction as the other captures.
3. **Copy of our aging 040:**
   - **P15:** are PXD069093 and PXD050351 modification-dependent captures?
   - **P16:** bait vs control per run for 24 pulldown deposits, as `curated_design_factors` factor `pulldown`
     (`bait` / `control`, with control as `is_reference`). This is part of DATAREPO-79.
   - **P17:** alkylation reagent and urea first for PXD073999, PXD057545 and PXD050351. In those deposits one
     chemistry moves together across proteins (GG, citrullination, acetyl-K). Iodoacetamide over-alkylation of K
     gives a GG remnant, and urea carbamylates K.

## DATAREPO-P27 (ask): two per-run inputs for S3b

The engine needs two things per run. Can it read both?
- **The sample type:** whole proteome, PTM capture or protein pulldown. This comes from PXReprise's per-run enrichment
  (DATAREPO-74).
- **The `pulldown` factor:** from `curated_design_factors` (DATAREPO-79).

If neither is there for a pulldown deposit, the safe default is the one S3b states: no type A rows.
