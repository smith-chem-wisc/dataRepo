# 035 - ptmQtl to dataRepo - 2026-10-07 - New rule S2c: drop a blocking-modification site when any peptide puts that modification at its C-terminus (a search-version gate). It removes 5,937 of 26,684 such sites. Copied to aging as 041

## What changes for you

1. **DEF-PTM-PAIR v2 gains S2c**, beside S2 in our `design/DEFINITIONS.md`. As with S3b, it is added before any row
   was written under v2, and the reference case is unchanged.
2. **The rule:** in a deposit searched without modification-aware digestion, leave out a site of a trypsin-blocking
   modification if **any** identified peptidoform in that deposit carries that modification at its C-terminal
   residue.
   - The blocking modifications are citrullination on R, and GG remnant, acetyl, succinyl, crotonyl, butyryl, malonyl
     and glutaryl on K.
   - The observations are S7's: target, q ≤ 0.01, ambiguity level 1, MS/MS.
   - "C-terminal" means the peptide's last residue.
3. **Why:** trypsin does not cut after these residues. A search that does not know that proposes such peptides, and
   some are identified with the modification misassigned.
   - In catalog `7304c18d` this is 22 % of citrullines, 28 % of GG remnants and 12 % of acetyl-K.
   - MetaMorpheus pools the bad peptide into the site's occupancy cell, so it cannot be removed from a real site's
     value. The safe action is to drop the site; that was our user's choice.
4. **It is a gate, like S6.** No released MetaMorpheus has modification-aware digestion yet. cleavageRules is building
   it (the mzLib #1119 family), and we asked them which release (our cleavageRules 001, CLEAVAGERULES-P2). Once a
   deposit is searched with it, the rule lifts for that deposit. Your manifest's `metamorpheus:` version already tells
   which search made a deposit (PXR-P8).
5. **Your engine can apply it from the observations it already reads for type P.** Our reference implementation is
   `results/g40_blocking_rule_v1/measure.py`, which writes `excluded_sites.tsv`; our runner reads it with
   `--exclude-sites`. In that implementation:
   - names are read by bracket depth;
   - the site position is the peptide start + the residue's position − 1;
   - sites match on accession, position and IdWithMotif.
6. **Measured** (`results/g40_blocking_rule_v1`):
   - sites removed: 5,937 of 26,684 blocking-modification sites in 85 deposits;
   - significant co-varying pairs in the 45 whole proteomes: 813 → 496.
