## The shape for P32 (proposed; schema 0.0.16, 1.4.0)

- **One pooled assay per design sample of several files.**
  - `assays.assay_kind = 'design_sample'` and `run_id` NULL; a run's assay is `assay_kind = 'run'`.
  - `sample_id` is the SDRF sample the design sample is, and `design_label` is MetaMorpheus's `{condition}_{biorep}`.
  - Its id is `<dataset_id>:design:<label>`.
- **`assay_runs`** lists the pooled assay's runs, one row each, with the design's `Fraction` and `Techrep` as written.
- **`ptm_stoichiometry`** rows go against the pooled assay with `denominator_grouping = sample_group`, beside the run
  rows. The two occupancy kinds combine differently (QuantProject 048, v3.10):
  - `DEF-OCC-COUNT` pools all the sample's PSMs into one numerator and one denominator;
  - `DEF-OCC-INT` sums each file's intensity shares over the sample's files, MS/MS only.
- **A sample of fractions of one injection:**

  ```sql
  SELECT a.assay_id FROM assays a JOIN assay_runs r USING (assay_id)
  WHERE a.assay_kind = 'design_sample'
  GROUP BY a.assay_id HAVING count(DISTINCT r.technical_replicate) = 1
  ```

- **Anything of yours that wants runs reads `assay_kind = 'run'` only** (QuantProject's note for you in their 048).
- A one-file sample stays on its run's assay, as since 1.3.1.
- If a design sample is not exactly one SDRF sample, its values are left out as today. In the ten designed searches
  stored, every one of the 181 samples is exactly one.

On today's store this gives PXD002467 its 48 samples of 2 fractions and PXD022196 its 4 samples of 8 bands, as you
counted. The seven ubiquitin captures' samples are stored too, and they pool two injections, so the query above leaves
them out.
