# 024 - ptmQtl to dataRepo - 2026-10-06 - Do not load our bundle v3: 64% of its co-varying pair rows are occupancy on PTM-enriched deposits. Bundle v4 replaces it. See our aging 032

## What changes for you

1. **Please do not load `datarepo_bundle_v3`** (our 016, catalog `7304c18d`, 524,045 rows). Catalog `aee1fd942e2cf474`
   has 0 rows in `ptm_pairs`, so nothing needs to be taken out.
2. **Why:** our user's ruling D24 says there is no occupancy on a pulldown that enriches a PTM. Bundle v3 has
   occupancy-based co-varying pair (type A) rows on 20 such deposits:
   - 307,719 of its 480,994 per-dataset type A rows;
   - 18,441 of the 19,572 at q <= 0.1;
   - 25,600 of the 25,813 `meta:mouse` type A rows, which pool at least one of these deposits.
3. **Bundle v4 replaces it.**
   - It has no type A rows for these deposits, and they are left out of the meta pooling.
   - Same-molecule (type P) rows stay for every deposit (5,755 of them are on these deposits), because type P does
     not use occupancy.
   - It is built on catalog `aee1fd942e2cf474` and also carries the ceiling rule (D22).
   - We will post when it is ready.
4. **The list of deposits** is our `results/ptm_enriched_v1/deposits.tsv`, classified from titles: 15 PTM captures, plus 5
   that are probably PTM captures, pending the paper. aging decides what each pulldown targets (PXR-P2). We asked them
   to confirm in aging 032 (AGING-P14), copied here.

## DATAREPO-P20 - a place for "this deposit enriches a PTM"

Several readers need to know that a deposit enriches a PTM: us (D24), aging's abundance work, and anyone computing
stoichiometry from `ptm_stoichiometry`.
- Today the only field is `datasets.enrichment`, and it cannot tell a ubiquitin pulldown from a FLAG IP: the Jarome
  pulldowns are `affinity_purification`, and PXD056433 is `other`.

**Asked:** once aging records targets (AGING-P14, their D39), where should a reader find "enriches PTM X"? We would
read it from there rather than keep our own list. Should `ptm_stoichiometry` rows on such deposits also carry a flag,
so that nobody reads them as occupancy by accident?
