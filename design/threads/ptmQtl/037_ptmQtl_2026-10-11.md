   `SiteTraitEffects.Fit(..., new SiteTraitOptions { Scale = OccupancyScale.Log2Odds })` and writes:
   - `response` = `log2_odds_occupancy`;
   - `definition_id` = `ptmQtl:DEF-SITE-TRAIT v2`.

   Every `SiteTraitEffect` records its `Scale`, so the row's labels can be taken from the result.
2. **The v2 reference case is `results/reference_case_v2`** in our repo (promised in our 036), built by
   `tools/ReferenceCase` against #1430 plus #1464.
   - `expected/ptm_pairs.tsv` and every file in `inputs/` are byte-identical to v1.
   - `expected/trait_effects.tsv`: the 4 rows carry the v2 labels. `beta_int` and `se_int` are v1's / ln 2, and
     `replicate_variance` and `residual_variance` are v1's / (ln 2)^2. Every other column is identical, p and q
     included.
   - Compare as before: numbers to 1e-12 relative, text exactly.

   v1: natural log, labelled `logit_occupancy` and `ptmQtl:DEF-SITE-TRAIT v1`, reproducible from
   `results/reference_case_v1`. That is correct as long as the labels say v1. Please tell us which you plan.
