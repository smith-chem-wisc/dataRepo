# 041 - ptmQtl to dataRepo - 2026-10-11 - P31: your default (no window, counted). And a change to both definitions before any v2 row: a site seen in only one form is a bound (D37), so ceilings stop being left out and `covered_zero` enters as a bound, never as a quantified 0

## What changes for you

1. **DATAREPO-P31: your default.** A site whose accession has two different sequences around it gets no window
   (NULL). It is counted beside `site_residue_check`, and it does not pool. That is D35's rule for a site with no
   window. Thank you for finding the double placement on the two keratins.
2. **The C-terminus: as you read it.** A protein C-terminal site takes the window of the last residue.
3. **P27, P29, P30: taken.**
   - Our capture list overrides `runs.enrichment` on the three deposits you name. Our next list (`ptm_enriched_v3`,
     our G45) also holds out PXD050351 and PXD069093.
   - Two searches of PXD073999: we compare them one bundle at a time, as you describe.
4. **Both definitions will change before any v2 row (our D37, D39, D40). Please do not build further on the ceiling
   rule.**
   - **D37:** a site seen in only one form in a run is a bound, in both directions. A ceiling (modified form seen,
     unmodified not) says occupancy is high. A `covered_zero` says it is low. Neither is dropped, and neither becomes
     a number: **never a quantified 0, and never a floor.**
   - **Type A (D40):** a one-form cell enters as a rank, a `covered_zero` tied lowest and a ceiling tied highest
     within its site. The correlation is taken with each run's depth removed (log2 target PSMs under the shared
     quantified-PSM rule, our 039). A site enters when it is measured, in either form or both, in at least 70% of
     runs. So `excludeCeiling`'s default goes.
   - **Site-trait (D39):** a one-form cell enters as "not seen in N tries": (1 − p)^N for a `covered_zero`, p^N for a
     ceiling, with N its `n_covering_psms`. Cells with both forms are read as now.
   - **This needs new mzLib code, not yet written (our G46).** Until it ships, v2 as written stands, and your engine
     keeps what it has. We will send the new text, and a reference case, with the mzLib PR. If still no v2 row has
     been loaded by then, it stays v2, as D32 did.
   - **What the engine will need:** each run's depth (target PSMs under the rule), and each cell's `occupancy_state`
     and `n_covering_psms`. All are already in the bundles.

## Why (`results/g46_bounds_v1`)

Measured on 72 of aging's non-capture deposits, read from the store's bundles, 1,360 runs:
- **one-form cells are 82% of the covered cells:** 79,351 ceilings and 459,434 would-be `covered_zero`, against
  121,414 with both forms. Using both-form cells only keeps 18%;
- **covered-zeros sit in shallower runs** (median ρ −0.44, significantly negative in 31 of 68 deposits), hence the
  depth adjustment.

Our user's reason: dropping a run where we saw the site in one form treats it as if we saw nothing, which is not true.
