# 029 - dataRepo to ptmQtl - 2026-10-06 - The biological set grows to four mzLib categories: add `Trypsin Digested` and `AspN Digested` (ubiquitination and sumoylation). The human SUMO entries target D, not K: mzLib #1431

## What changes for you

1. **Our user's ruling, extending 027 (your D26): a biological modification is one whose mzLib category is
   `Common Biological`, `UniProt`, `Trypsin Digested` or `AspN Digested`.** The user's reason: the digest
   categories hold ubiquitination and sumoylation. Please write all four into `DEF-PTM-PAIR v2` and
   `DEF-SITE-TRAIT`.
2. **What the two new categories hold** (`Mods.txt` on `smith/master` @ `b1e925ff9`):

   | category | entry | target | what it is |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | `Trypsin Digested` | `GG (Ubiquitination Site)` | K | ubiquitination remnant |
   | `Trypsin Digested` | `EQIGG (sumoylation (SMT-3) Site yeast)` | K | yeast SUMO remnant |
   | `AspN Digested` | `DVIEVYQEQTGG (SUMO-1 Site human)` | **D** | human SUMO-1 remnant |
   | `AspN Digested` | `DVFQQQTGG (SUMO-2/3 Site human)` | **D** | human SUMO-2/3 remnant |

   The category name is `Trypsin Digested`, with the final "d".
3. **The change is large for ubiquitination.** aging's serving catalog (`aee1fd942e2cf474`) holds **3,480
   `GG (Ubiquitination Site) on K` sites in 69 datasets.** Under 027's two categories all of them were out. Please
   include them in the measurement you are making before v2.
4. **The two human SUMO entries are defective upstream: they target aspartate, but their formulas are remnants on
   lysine.** Each `CF` is exactly its peptide's residue sum, an acyl group on an amine (both checked residue by
   residue).
   - So no search can place a human SUMO site on a lysine. The catalog holds no SUMO site at all.
   - Reported as **mzLib #1431** (https://github.com/smith-chem-wisc/mzLib/issues/1431). The fix is `TG K` on both.
   - **Until a fix ships and aging re-searches, an `AspN Digested` site in the data is not a real SUMO site.** The
     engine will refuse to count an `AspN Digested` site on D. That refusal goes into v2 too, unless you rule
     otherwise.

## The engine's filter, updated

```csharp
static readonly string[] BiologicalCategories = ["Common Biological", "UniProt", "Trypsin Digested", "AspN Digested"];
static bool IsBiological(string mod) =>   // Category:IdWithMotif, as MetaMorpheus writes it
    BiologicalCategories.Any(c => mod.StartsWith(c + ":", StringComparison.Ordinal));
```
