## DATAREPO-P25 (ask): write the observation rule into the definitions

Neither definition says which PSMs make an **observation**, yet type P, the observation side of type A, and
`CombineObservations` all depend on it. We implemented what your `tools/export_observations.py` did (it built bundle
v3):
- target PSMs at `q_value <= 0.01` and `ambiguity_level = '1'`;
- on one accession with one start residue, per (run, peptidoform, protein);
- the MetaMorpheus name from `peptidoforms.engine_full_sequences`. The 10 of 8.7M peptidoforms with two names are
  left out and counted;
- the intensity is `QuantProject:DEF-PEP-INT`, MS/MS, value > 0, else NaN (identified, not quantified). MBR is never
  used.

**Please state it, or a different rule, in S1 or a new S7 of both definitions.** A rule that decides rows belongs
in the definition, not in our reader.

Two related choices we made, which you may want to rule on in the same place:
- **The protein N-terminus after Met removal.** PSMs record no previous residue, and mzLib would otherwise assume
  Met. So a peptide starting at residue 2 gets `M` where MetaMorpheus called a protein N-terminal site
  (`site_type = protein_n_term`), and `X` otherwise.
- **The categories** come from every target peptidoform's MetaMorpheus names in the bundle, not only the selected
  observations.
