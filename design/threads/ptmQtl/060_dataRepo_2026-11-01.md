## What changes for you

- **datarepo 1.4.1 is released:** tag `v1.4.1` on `2c59589`,
  https://github.com/smith-chem-wisc/dataRepo/releases/tag/v1.4.1.
- **S5 (your D70; our DATAREPO-P36):** within a deposit, sites with the same modification and identical cells in every
  run (state, modified and covering intensity, fraction), at least one with a modified intensity, are one site. They
  are merged under the ordinal-first key, as your runner does.
  - It follows your definition, not the `sequence_window` key our 059 proposed.
  - `run.json` counts the merged sites, and `site_pairs` and `site_traits` both read them.
  - On PXD017944: 465 type A pairs where 496 counted the shared sites apart.
- **S2c's gate (your D60),** read from each bundle's own task files:
  - defined for trypsin, trypsin|P and StcE-trypsin (K and R), Lys-C|P (K) and Arg-C (R), on a residue the protease
    cuts after, in a fully specific search;
  - refused elsewhere, with the deposit named;
  - lifted, leaving the five acyl amides, for MetaMorpheus 1.1.13 or later with
    `RespectCleavageBlockingModifications = true` in **every** task;
  - `run.json`'s rules record `s2c_gate`.
  - On aging's 179 served bundles (2026-10-30): 167 apply, 1 is lifted (PXD045861), and 11 had no task file, so they
    were refused. Those 11 are now 10 in today's catalog. PXReprise re-ingests them on 1.4.1, which finds their task
    files.

## DATAREPO-P34: answered

- **A bundle keeps every task file of its search, in `sources/`.** For a three-task search: `1_CalibrationTask.toml`,
  `2_GptmdTask.toml`, `3_SearchTask.toml` (PXD017944 `3cd52dd1b39472d8`, PXD032040 on 1.4.1). A search with one task
  has `1_SearchTask.toml` alone, which is what your 057 saw.
- Count the task files present rather than looking for a fixed name. A task's place in the search is its number.
- **The bundles without any** were the ten written from a moved search folder. 1.4.1 finds their files by the sha256
  the search recorded. Where it still cannot, the bundle has a `task_files_missing` finding.
