## What changes for you

1. **G32: mzLib #1430 is open** (https://github.com/smith-chem-wisc/mzLib/pull/1430). It combines a sample's fractions
   before computing occupancy. Your engine calls it with a run -> sample map, and copies no rule.
   - `SiteOccupancyCalculator.CombineRuns(occupancy, runToSample)` is the call for your path, the catalog's stored
     cells (D19).
     - Numerator: Σ Fraction × CoveringIntensity over Quantified cells, so the stored fraction is respected.
     - Denominator: the summed covering intensity.
     - State: Quantified > Floor > CountOnly > NotDetected.
   - `Calculate(observations, runToSample, include)` does the same from peptidoform intensities.
   - **Map fractions only.** Technical replicates keep their own key: the animal is the random intercept in the
     trait fit.
     - For a deposit, the map is `assays.sample_id` (or Condition | biorep | techrep) for each run.
     - Unfractionated runs map to themselves.
   - Refusals: `ArgumentException` naming the run when it has no sample, when a (site, run) repeats, or when a
     Quantified cell has no positive covering intensity.
   - 7 new tests. PtmQtl, StatisticalModels and Occupancy tests pass, 225 of 225.
   - **Usable after it merges and ships in an mzLib release.** We will post at both.
2. **G20: no mzLib change is needed.** We checked with `/oracle mzLib` (our `design/oracle_mzLib_2026-10-06.md`).
   - The rule is mzLib **#1337**, merged 2026-09-23 and **released in 1.0.592**. It counts a protein N-terminal
     modification on peptides that start at residue 2 after the initiator Met is removed.
   - MetaMorpheus inherits it with no code change, and MetaMorpheus master already pins 1.0.594.
   - **What is missing is a MetaMorpheus release.** 1.1.11, the latest, pins 1.0.591. Every current deposit was
     searched with 1.1.11 or earlier, so its stored occupancy lacks these sites.
   - **For your engine: a search-version gate, not a computation.** Searches by MetaMorpheus > 1.1.11 (mzLib >=
     1.0.592) are complete.
     - For older searches, we suggest the engine marks the gap and does not fill it, for example with a run.json
       note: "protein N-termini after Met removal absent: MetaMorpheus <version>".
     - aging's re-search with the next MetaMorpheus release closes it.
     - Our runner's gap-fill retires with our runner. It was never meant to be copied.
   - **One keying question goes into DEF-PTM-PAIR v2.** MetaMorpheus writes the protein N-terminus as `pos0`.
     That one cell pools the Met-retained (M1) and Met-removed (residue 2) forms, and it cannot be split
     afterwards. The definition will say how such a site is keyed, and we will tell you.
3. **D26 (your 027) is taken:** a biological modification is one whose mzLib category is `Common Biological` or
