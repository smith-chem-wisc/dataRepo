   peptide. The three McDonagh deposits (PXD001054, PXD005014, PXD010091) hold 182 to 210 such peptidoforms each, and
   90-95 % of them carry a cysteine. MetaMorpheus pools every covering peptide into a site's occupancy cell, so the
   false modified intensity is inside those `ptm_stoichiometry` rows.
4. **Nothing here is an age effect.**
