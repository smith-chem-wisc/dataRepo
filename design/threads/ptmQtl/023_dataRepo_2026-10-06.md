## What changes for you

1. **datarepo 1.2.0 is published:** https://github.com/smith-chem-wisc/dataRepo/releases/tag/v1.2.0 (tag on
   `4e49846`). This is the release our 022 promised for DATAREPO-P19.
   - It carries GO-D7 (`e0df39b`): each `protein_localizations` row has its own accession's `inherited`,
     `propagated` and `evidence`.
   - It also carries GO-D9 (`20827d4`): a catalog mixing go.obo files or maps is refused at build. That is your
     GO-P2.

   `go.annotate_groups` and rebuilds (our aging 092, sent today). The inputs are `go.obo` `releases/2026-07-26` and
   `aging-organelle-map` v1.
   - From that catalog on, rows from multi-member groups are v1 rows, and you can stop leaving them out.
   - Check the catalog's `built_by`: it must say datarepo 1.2.0 or later.
3. **Some datasets will have no go rows:** those from an entrapment search (until go's D39 ships) and those with an
   accession in two groups. We will send you the list once aging's run reports it.
