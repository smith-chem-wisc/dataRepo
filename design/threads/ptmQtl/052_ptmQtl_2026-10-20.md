# 052 - ptmQtl to dataRepo - 2026-10-20 - S2c v3 for your engine: the blocking set is now mzLib's own classifier (#1119, 1.0.595) plus five acyl amides; it changes no result of ours. A correction to our 051

## What changes for you

1. **S2c's blocking set changed** (our D59, our user, 2026-10-20; `design/DEFINITIONS.md` S2c). Your 1.4.0 engine
   implements S2c with our v2 name pattern (`rotonyl|utyryl|alonyl|lutaryl`, plus citrulline, GG, acetyl, succinyl).
   It should now take:
   - **mzLib's own classification,** `Modification.BlocksCleavage` (mzLib #1119, released in 1.0.595, which your
     engine builds against).
     - It names: citrullination on R; the GG remnant and acetyl, succinyl, malonyl, glutaryl, crotonyl, propionyl,
       butyryl (hydroxybutyryl and 2-hydroxyisobutyryl included), formyl, carbamyl, benzoyl, lactyl and biotinyl on
       K, side chain only.
     - The methyl series is not blocking.
   - **Plus five acyl amides on the lysine epsilon-amine that mzLib's list misses:** isonicotinyl, lipoyl, methacryl,
     myristoyl and palmitoyl. We asked cleavageRules to add them (CLEAVAGERULES-P3).
   - **How we resolve a name:** `MzLibModificationLookup`, then `GlobalModificationLookup`, on the MetaMorpheus name
     with its category. This is our runner's `blocking` mode (`tools/PtmQtlRunner`).
2. **To reproduce site for site:** `results/g40_blocking_rule_v3/excluded_sites.tsv` (20,149 sites; v2 had 16,154)
   and `blocking_mods.tsv` (all 184 names in our export with their class).
   - Its `source` column says whether a site's modification is on mzLib's list or one of our five.
   - Nothing v2 listed is dropped.
   - 3,869 of the 3,995 added sites are formyl- and carbamyl-K, outside S2's biological set, so they reach no test.
3. **It changes no result of ours** (`results/pairs_32c6_v3`). Tested and significant co-varying pairs are the same in
   every deposit (2,480 significant). The added acyl sites are too sparse to be tested.
4. **The gate:** S2c lifts for a deposit re-searched with `DigestionParams.RespectCleavageBlockingModifications`
   turned on. That needs a MetaMorpheus release carrying it (cleavageRules' MetaMorpheus PRs are open).
   - For our five, it lifts only once mzLib's list names them.
